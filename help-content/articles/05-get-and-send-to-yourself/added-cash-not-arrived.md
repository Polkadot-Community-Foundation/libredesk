Most of the time it's still on its way. How long it takes depends on how you added ᴄᴀꜱʜ: crypto usually takes 1–20 minutes, card payments a few minutes, and bank transfers 1–2 business days. Sometimes the external provider might have additional checks or delays as part of their business process. 

## Check these first

1. Tap **Pocket** in the dock, tap your ᴄᴀꜱʜ purse, then tap **Get**. Recent ones are listed under "Topping up". Tap one to see where it's up to.
2. Check its status:
   - "Waiting for your deposit", "Waiting for your payment" or "Waiting for your bank transfer": the app hasn't seen your crypto, card payment or transfer yet.
   - "On its way" or "Adding to your balance": it has arrived and is turning into ᴄᴀꜱʜ.
   - "Delayed, don't pay again": the payment provider is retrying. Don't pay a second time.
   - "Added": it's done. Check your Total in the ᴄᴀꜱʜ purse.
   - "Payment failed", "Refunded" or "Expired": see [Adding ᴄᴀꜱʜ didn't work or was refunded](adding-cash-didnt-work.md).
3. Check your internet connection. "Trying to reconnect" means the app hasn't had an update for a while.

If you see "This is taking longer than usual. Your top-up will continue automatically", you don't need to do anything. It carries on by itself.

## If you sent crypto

- Some networks need several confirmations first. You'll see "Confirming your [token] deposit" and a count such as "Confirmation 2 of 6".
- In your other crypto wallet, check that you sent the token and network you picked, to the address shown, and the exact amount.
- If it says "This top-up expired because no funds arrived in time." but you did send crypto, contact support with the details below.
- If a different amount or token arrived, see [The wrong amount or token arrived](wrong-amount-or-token.md).

## If you paid by card

Card payments usually take a few minutes. If you see "We can't confirm your payment with [provider]. Check your card statement before you pay again.", look at your card statement before you try again.

## If you sent a bank transfer

Bank transfers take 1–2 business days, and sometimes a few days. Check that you sent the exact amount shown and included the reference.

If the provider doesn't receive it, you'll see "We haven't received your bank transfer. If it arrives later, [provider] returns it to your bank account."

## Still need help?

Email us at [help@pcf.network](mailto:help@pcf.network), or in the app tap **Settings** > **Legal & Support** > **Contact us**. Tell us your app version (it's at the bottom of **Settings**) and the exact message you saw.

Include the "Reference" from the payment's screen as well. Tap it to copy it.
