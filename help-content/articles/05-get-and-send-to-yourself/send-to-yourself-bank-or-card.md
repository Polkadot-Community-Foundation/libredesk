You can cash out ᴄᴀꜱʜ to your bank account or card in [supported countries]. The payment provider checks your ID first. Bank payouts usually take 1–2 business days.

## Send ᴄᴀꜱʜ to your bank or card

1. Tap **Pocket** in the dock, tap your ᴄᴀꜱʜ purse, then tap **Send**.
2. Tap **Send to yourself** ("Bank account or crypto wallet").
3. On "Send money out", choose **Bank** or **Card**, type how much ᴄᴀꜱʜ to send, then tap **Continue**.
4. Choose your country on "Choose payout country".
5. Check the summary: "From your balance inc. fees", your "Payout country", the "Min. Payout" and when it "Arrives". Then tap **Continue**.
6. Complete the ID check on the provider's page ("Verify with the provider").
7. When the list shows "Verified. Open it to pay from your balance", open it and confirm.

You'll see progress messages such as "Moving your money to the payment provider" and "Sending the money to your bank". Bank payouts take 1–2 business days. A card payout can take up to 2 days to show, depending on your bank.

The amount you receive depends on the exchange rate when the payout is made.

## Fees

You'll see every fee before you confirm: "Provider fee", "Network fee" and "Service fee". The "Swapping fees" line shows "Variable" because it depends on the rate at the time.

## If Bank or Card shows "Soon"

That option isn't available for you right now. You can send ᴄᴀꜱʜ to a crypto wallet instead. See [Send ᴄᴀꜱʜ to your crypto wallet](send-to-yourself-crypto.md).

## Who handles the payout

Bank and card payouts go through Meld, which picks a payment provider for your country. The screen shows "Powered by Meld". The provider does the ID check and sends the money to your bank or card.
