What happens depends on the network you sent on. On Polkadot, the app asks whether to convert what arrived or give it back to you. On Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana and Tron, the exchange is done by Chainflip, and its rules decide what happens.

## If you sent on Polkadot (DOT, USDC or USDT)

The app notices the difference and shows either "[token] arrived instead of [token]" or "Less arrived than asked". 
The screen lists what was "Asked", what "Arrived", and what "You'll get" if you carry on.

- To convert what arrived, tap **Continue with $[amount] ᴄᴀꜱʜ**.
- To get it back, tap **Recover my funds**, then follow [Get your crypto back after a refund](crypto-refund.md).
- If too little arrived, you can also send the rest to the same address.

If you see "This can't be converted to CASH. You can take it back.", tap **Recover my funds**.

If more arrived than you asked for, the app converts what arrived.

## If you sent on Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana or Tron

On these networks, the app doesn't ask whether to convert or take it back. Chainflip exchanges what arrives if it can. 

- If Chainflip can't exchange it, your crypto goes to a refund address. See [Get your crypto back after a refund](crypto-refund.md).
- If what arrived was below Chainflip's minimum, you'll see "Chainflip doesn't return deposits below the minimum."

> [!WARNING]
> Crypto below Chainflip's minimum isn't returned. Please ensure you always send the correct amount of crypto. <!-- REVIEW F20 -->

## If you sent on a different network

If you sent on a network other than the one shown on screen, the app may not see your crypto, and it may not be possible to get it back. Always double-check the network and amount.
