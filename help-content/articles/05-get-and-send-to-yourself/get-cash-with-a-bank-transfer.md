If you're in the UK or one of 12 euro countries, you can add ᴄᴀꜱʜ with a bank transfer. 
You can send the money from your own banking app to a bank account, and you'll receive ᴄᴀꜱʜ in 1–2 business days. This method is slower than getting ᴄᴀꜱʜ with a payment card, but it's typically much cheaper. 
The payment provider will likely ask to check your identity documents and request information about you to facilitate the transaction. This is the [ID check](id-check.md).

## Countries

- the UK, through Open Banking
- 12 euro countries, through SEPA: Austria, Belgium, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Ireland, Italy, Luxembourg, Netherlands, Portugal and Spain

## Add ᴄᴀꜱʜ with a bank transfer

1. Tap **Pocket** in the dock, tap your ᴄᴀꜱʜ purse, then tap **Get**.
2. On "Top up funds", tap **Bank**.
3. Type how much ᴄᴀꜱʜ you want, then tap **Continue**.
4. Check the "Top up via bank" screen. It shows "Send this exact amount inc. fees", your "Payment country", what "You'll receive", and when it "Arrives" ("1–2 business days").
5. Tap **Continue**. The payment provider's page opens inside the app. Follow its steps, including the ID check, to get the bank details to send to.
6. In your banking app, send the exact amount shown to those details. If there's a reference, include it.
7. Back in Polkadot, tap **I've sent funds**. The button becomes available after a few seconds.

The screen then says "If you've sent the money from your bank, it's on its way. Bank transfers can take a few days. This page will update when it arrives." When it arrives, your ᴄᴀꜱʜ is added.

The amount of ᴄᴀꜱʜ you get depends on the exchange rate when your transfer arrives. If the rate moved, the screen shows "Rates changed while your money was on its way" and the new amount. 

## If the transfer details expire

You'll see "Transfer details expired". If you've already sent the money, it will still arrive. If you haven't, tap **Back to top-ups** and start again.

## If your transfer doesn't arrive

If the provider doesn't receive it, the screen says "We haven't received your bank transfer. If it arrives later, [provider] returns it to your bank account." See [I added ᴄᴀꜱʜ but it hasn't arrived](added-cash-not-arrived.md).
