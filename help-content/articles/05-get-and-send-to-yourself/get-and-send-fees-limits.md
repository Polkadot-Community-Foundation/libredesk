When you're getting ᴄᴀꜱʜ in and out of the app, there are some fees and limits that you need to be aware of.
If a fee is charged, it's always displayed to you first, so you can see any fees for what you're about to do. 

The Polkadot app is free to use, and it's always free to hold and send ᴄᴀꜱʜ. These fees are only ever charged for moving ᴄᴀꜱʜ into or out of the app.

## Fees

| What you're doing                                     | Fees you may see                                                           |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Adding ᴄᴀꜱʜ with crypto                               | Built into the amount the screen asks you to send.                         |
| Adding ᴄᴀꜱʜ by card                                   | "Provider fee", "Network fee", "Mint fee", "Service fee"                   |
| Adding ᴄᴀꜱʜ by bank transfer                          | "Provider fee", "Network fee", "Mint fee", "Service fee"                   |
| Sending to yourself: crypto wallet on Polkadot        | "Network fee", taken in ᴄᴀꜱʜ                                               |
| Sending to yourself: crypto wallet on another network | "Network fee", "Swap fee", "Service fee"                                   |
| Sending to yourself: bank or card                     | "Provider fee", "Network fee", "Service fee", "Swapping fees" ("Variable") |
| Sending ᴄᴀꜱʜ to someone in your chats                 | None                                                                       |
| Holding ᴄᴀꜱʜ                                          | None                                                                       |

On the card and bank fee screens, fees that are zero aren't listed. 
The "Rate" line shows what $1 ᴄᴀꜱʜ is worth in the other currency, for example "$1 CASH ≈ €0.93". The final amount can change a little with the exchange rate.

## Limits

These are the general limits for each time you add ᴄᴀꜱʜ or send it to yourself:

| What you're doing | Minimum  | Maximum                                          |
| ----------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Add ᴄᴀꜱʜ (Get)    | $10 ᴄᴀꜱʜ | $5,000 ᴄᴀꜱʜ                                      |
| Send to yourself  | $10 ᴄᴀꜱʜ | $2,000 ᴄᴀꜱʜ, or the ᴄᴀꜱʜ you have if that's less |

Your own limits depend on your country and on how you add or cash out your ᴄᴀꜱʜ, so they may be narrower than this:

- For card and bank transfers, the minimum and maximum depend on your country. The country list shows "Minimum for this country is [amount]".
- For crypto, some networks and tokens have a higher minimum. They show "Minimum for this network is $[amount] ᴄᴀꜱʜ".

The screen always shows your limits before you continue. Under the amount you'll see lines such as "Minimum $10 CASH", "Maximum $5,000 CASH" or "Not enough CASH".
