You can move ᴄᴀꜱʜ out to your own crypto wallet at any time. You choose the network and token, paste your address, and the app shows every fee before you confirm.

## What you can receive

| Network | Tokens |
|---|---|
| Polkadot | DOT, USDC, USDT |
| Bitcoin | BTC |
| Ethereum | ETH, USDC, USDT |
| Solana | SOL, USDC, USDT |
| Tron | TRX, USDT |


## Send ᴄᴀꜱʜ to your crypto wallet

1. Tap **Pocket** in the dock, tap your ᴄᴀꜱʜ purse, then tap **Send**.
2. Tap **Send to yourself** ("Bank account or crypto wallet"). It shows while the search box is empty.
3. On "Send money out", choose **Crypto**, type how much ᴄᴀꜱʜ to send, then tap **Continue**. The screen shows how much you have as "Available".
4. Choose the network, then the token.
5. Tap **Paste** to add your crypto wallet address. If the app shows "Incorrect address" or "This is not [network] address", check that you copied the right address for that network.
6. Once the address is accepted, the screen says "Funds sent to the wrong network can't be recovered." Check the network, then tap **Continue**.
7. Check the summary: "From your balance inc. fees", what "You'll receive", and "To this address on [network] Network".
8. Tap **Confirm withdrawal**, then approve the payment on the Polkadot screen that opens. Approve it within 30 minutes, or the request runs out and nothing is sent.

You'll see progress messages such as "Converting your Cash" and "Your [token] is on its way to your wallet". When it's done, the screen shows the amount that left, for example "-$50 ᴄᴀꜱʜ". Tap **Close**.

> [!WARNING]
> Crypto sent to the wrong address or the wrong network can't be recovered. Copy the address from your crypto wallet and check the network before you confirm.

## Fees

Sending to Polkadot has only a "Network fee", taken in ᴄᴀꜱʜ. Sending to Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana or Tron has a "Network fee", a "Swap fee" and a "Service fee". You'll see "Total fees" and what "You'll receive" before you confirm.

Cashing out isn't private in the way ᴄᴀꜱʜ is. The crypto arrives in your crypto wallet like any other payment on that network, and anyone looking at your wallet address will be able to see the crypto arrive.
