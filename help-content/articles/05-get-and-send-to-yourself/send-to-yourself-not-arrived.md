Most of the time it's still on its way. Crypto usually arrives within minutes, depending on the network, and bank payouts take 1–2 business days. If something stopped it, the app says what happened to your ᴄᴀꜱʜ.

## Check these first

1. Tap **Pocket** in the dock, tap your ᴄᴀꜱʜ purse, tap **Send**, then tap **Send to yourself**. Recent ones are listed under "Sending". Tap one to see where it's up to.
2. Check its status: "Withdrawal initiated", "Converting to [token]…", "Sent" or "Cancelled".
3. If it says "Sent", open your crypto wallet and check the network you chose.

If you see "The conversion is taking longer than usual. It will continue automatically.", you don't need to do anything. Check back in a few hours.

## If it didn't go through

| You see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "This withdrawal expired because the payment never arrived." or "This withdrawal took too long to pay for. Nothing was sent, so you can try again." | The payment wasn't approved in time, so nothing left your ᴄᴀꜱʜ. Start again and approve it straight away. |
| "The network refused the transaction. You can try again." | Tap **Try again**. |
| "CASH can't be redeemed right now. Your funds are safe; you can try again or sell on the pool." | Tap **Try again** later. You can also tap **Sell on the pool for about [amount]**, which shows what you'd get instead. <!-- REVIEW F39: explain "sell on the pool" in plain words and confirm the trade-off. --> |
| "The rate moved too far to complete the conversion. Your DOT is waiting, you'll need to move it yourself." | Tap **Return funds** and follow [Get your crypto back after a refund](crypto-refund.md). |
| "Something went wrong with this withdrawal." | Contact the payment provider you used. |

## If you cashed out to a bank or card

- Bank payouts take 1–2 business days. A card payout can take up to 2 days to show, depending on your bank.
- "The price moved too far while you verified. Nothing was taken from your balance." or "This sale expired before it was completed. Nothing was taken from your balance.": your ᴄᴀꜱʜ is still in your purse. Start again.
- "Your bank didn't accept the payout." or "The provider couldn't complete your payout.": contact the payment provider you used. 

## If you sent to the wrong address

Crypto sent to the wrong address or network can't be recovered. Nobody can reverse it, including Polkadot support.

## Still need help?

Get in touch with the payment provider that you were transacting with for further support.
