If a card or bank payment doesn't go through, either no money is taken or the payment provider sends it back. If your crypto can't be converted, it goes to a refund address that the app keeps for you, and you can move it from there. The screen tells you which one happened.

## If you paid by card

| You see | What it means |
|---|---|
| "Your bank declined the payment. No money was taken." | Your bank said no. Try another card, or check with your bank. |
| "The payment didn't go through. No money was taken." or "We didn't receive your payment. No money was taken." | Nothing was charged. You can try again. |
| "[provider] couldn't complete your top-up and is returning [amount] to your card." | The money is on its way back to your card. How long it takes depends on your bank. |
| "Payment refunded" | The money has gone back to your card. |
| "We can't confirm your payment with [provider]. Check your card statement before you pay again." | Check your statement before you try again, so you don't pay twice. |

## If you sent a bank transfer

If you see "[provider] couldn't accept your bank transfer and is returning the money to your bank account.", the money goes back to your bank. If you see "We can't confirm your bank transfer with [provider]. Check with your bank before you send the money again.", ask your bank whether it left your account before you send it again.

## If you sent crypto

- "The exchange didn't go through. Your [token] is being returned, and you can move it to your own wallet." Your crypto goes to a refund address, minus fees. Tap **Get your [token] back** and follow [Get your crypto back after a refund](crypto-refund.md).
- "Your [token] was below the minimum, so Chainflip couldn't exchange it. Chainflip doesn't return deposits below the minimum."
- "Your [token] was too small to pay the network fees, so Chainflip couldn't exchange it." 
- "Chainflip couldn't accept your [token]", "The exchange didn't go through, and Chainflip couldn't return your [token]" or "Chainflip exchanged your [token] but couldn't send it": contact support with the reference.


## If the conversion didn't finish

Some messages end with "Your money is safe, and you won't need to pay again.", for example "We couldn't convert your money to Cash." If there's a **Try again** button, tap it. If there isn't, contact us. Either way, you don't need to pay again.

## If you couldn't start

- "Couldn't get the rate": tap **Try again** in a moment. If you then see "Still not working", try a different amount, change the country, or tap **Use crypto**.
- "Not available for this payment method in your region ([country]). Try another method.": see [Where can I use a card or bank transfer?](where-card-and-bank-work.md)

## Still need help?

Email us at [help@pcf.network](mailto:help@pcf.network), or in the app tap **Settings** > **Legal & Support** > **Contact us**. Tell us your app version (it's at the bottom of **Settings**) and the exact message you saw.

Include the "Reference" from the payment's screen as well. Tap it to copy it.
