You can add ᴄᴀꜱʜ with crypto, a card or a bank transfer. 
Tap **Pocket** in the dock, tap your ᴄᴀꜱʜ purse, then tap **Get**. 
The screen that opens is titled "Top up funds".

| | Crypto | Card | Bank transfer |
|---|---|---|---|
| ID check | No | Yes, done by the payment provider | Yes, done by the payment provider |
| How long it takes | Minutes, depending on the network | "A few minutes" | "1–2 business days" |
| Fees | Built into the amount you send | Listed before you pay | Listed before you pay |

Card payments work in [supported countries], and bank transfers work in the UK and 12 euro countries. 

In general, you can add $10 to $5,000 ᴄᴀꜱʜ at a time. Your country and how you pay can change this, and the screen shows your limits before you continue.

Once it's in, your ᴄᴀꜱʜ is private, and sending it to people in your chats has no fees.

- [Get ᴄᴀꜱʜ with crypto](get-cash-with-crypto.md): send BTC, ETH, SOL, TRX, DOT, USDC or USDT from another crypto wallet.
- [Get ᴄᴀꜱʜ with a card](get-cash-with-a-card.md): pay by card on the payment provider's page inside the app.
- [Get ᴄᴀꜱʜ with a bank transfer](get-cash-with-a-bank-transfer.md): send a transfer from your bank in the UK or the euro countries listed.
- [Where can I use a card or bank transfer?](where-card-and-bank-work.md): the countries for each option.
- [Why do I need an ID check?](id-check.md): what the payment provider checks, and why crypto doesn't need it.
- [Fees and limits](get-and-send-fees-limits.md): every fee, and where to see the limits.
- [I added ᴄᴀꜱʜ but it hasn't arrived](added-cash-not-arrived.md): check where it's up to.

If you see "Get ᴄᴀꜱʜ isn't available right now. Try again later.", wait a few minutes and tap **Get** again. If you've just restored your account, **Get** appears once you've finished checking your ᴄᴀꜱʜ. See [Changing phones and accessing your ᴄᴀꜱʜ](../04-cash-and-payments/changing-phones.md).

To move ᴄᴀꜱʜ out to a crypto wallet or a bank, see [Send ᴄᴀꜱʜ to your crypto wallet](send-to-yourself-crypto.md) or [Send ᴄᴀꜱʜ to your bank or card](send-to-yourself-bank-or-card.md).
