Card payments and bank transfers go through payment providers, and those providers check who you are before they move money between your bank and ᴄᴀꜱʜ. Adding ᴄᴀꜱʜ with crypto, or sending it to your own crypto wallet, doesn't need an ID check.

## How it works

When you add ᴄᴀꜱʜ by card or bank transfer, or send it to your bank or card, the app opens the payment provider's page inside the app. The provider runs the ID check there. It may ask for a photo of your ID and use your camera. 

The Polkadot app doesn't do the check itself. The details you enter go to the provider.

When you cash out to a bank or card, the step is called "Verify with the provider". While it's waiting for you, the list shows "Waiting for you to finish with the provider". Once you're done, it shows "Verified. Open it to pay from your balance".

## What this means for you

The provider knows who paid and how much, so adding or cashing out ᴄᴀꜱʜ by card or bank isn't private. Once it's in, your ᴄᴀꜱʜ is private. Sending it to people in your chats doesn't involve the provider.

If you'd rather not do an ID check, use crypto instead. See [Get ᴄᴀꜱʜ with crypto](get-cash-with-crypto.md) and [Send ᴄᴀꜱʜ to your crypto wallet](send-to-yourself-crypto.md).
