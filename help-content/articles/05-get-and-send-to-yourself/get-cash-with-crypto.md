Send crypto from another crypto wallet and the app converts it to ᴄᴀꜱʜ for you. You don't need an ID check, and it usually takes minutes, depending on the network.

## What you can send

| Network  | Tokens                  |
| -------- | ----------------------- |
| Polkadot | DOT, USDC, USDT, dotUSD |
| Bitcoin  | BTC                     |
| Ethereum | ETH, USDC, USDT         |
| Solana   | SOL, USDC, USDT         |
| Tron     | TRX, USDT               |

## Add ᴄᴀꜱʜ with crypto

1. Tap **Pocket** in the dock, tap your ᴄᴀꜱʜ purse, then tap **Get**.
2. On "Top up funds", check that **Crypto** is selected.
3. Type how much ᴄᴀꜱʜ you want, or tap a preset amount, then tap **Continue**.
4. Choose the network you'll send from, then choose the token.
5. The next screen shows a QR code, "Send this exact amount" and "To this address on [network] Network". Tap either one to copy it.
6. In your other crypto wallet, send that exact amount of that token, on that network, to that address.

Once your crypto arrives, you'll see progress messages such as "Confirming your [token] deposit" and "Exchanging your [token]". When the ᴄᴀꜱʜ is added, the screen shows the amount, for example "+$50 ᴄᴀꜱʜ". Tap **Close**.

You can leave this screen while you wait. To come back to it, tap **Get** and find it in the list under "Topping up". 

## Before you send

> [!WARNING]
> Send only the token you picked, on the network you picked. Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana and Tron payments are exchanged by Chainflip, and Chainflip doesn't return amounts below its minimum.


- Each address is for this one payment only and works for about 24 hours. If nothing arrives in time, you'll see "This top-up expired because no funds arrived in time." Start a new one instead of reusing an old address.
- A greyed-out network or token shows why: "Not available yet", "Not available right now", "Amount too small" or "Minimum for this network is $[amount] ᴄᴀꜱʜ". Try a larger amount or another network.
- The fees are built into the amount the screen asks you to send, so there are no separate fee lines.

The crypto payment you send from your other crypto wallet is recorded on its own network, as usual. Once it's in, your ᴄᴀꜱʜ is private.
