Card payments and bank transfers work in some countries only. Where they aren't available, you can add or cash out ᴄᴀꜱʜ with crypto instead, which doesn't need an ID check.

## How the app picks your country

The app suggests a country based on your internet connection and your phone's language settings. You can change it on the payment screen by tapping **Payment country** (or **Payout country** when you cash out). 

If the option isn't available in your country, you'll see "Card transfer isn't available in this region" or "Bank transfer isn't available in this region". You can still add ᴄᴀꜱʜ with crypto. Other payment methods, such as local payment apps, aren't supported.

Minimum amounts for card and bank depend on the country. The country list shows each one as "Minimum for this country is [amount]".
