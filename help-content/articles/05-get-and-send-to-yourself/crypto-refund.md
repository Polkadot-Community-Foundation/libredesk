When the app can't convert your crypto to ᴄᴀꜱʜ, the crypto goes back to a refund address that belongs to that one payment. The app shows you the key for that address, so you can move the crypto into a crypto wallet you use.

This key only works for that refund. It isn't your recovery phrase, and it doesn't open your Polkadot account.

## Open the refund details

1. Tap **Pocket** in the dock, tap your ᴄᴀꜱʜ purse, then tap **Get**.
2. Tap the payment in your list.
3. Tap **Get your [token] back**. If the app asked you about a different amount or token, tap **Recover my funds** instead.
4. The status line shows where your crypto is, from "Your [token] is being returned to your recovery address." to "...is back at your recovery address." Wait until it's back.

## Move crypto on Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana or Tron

1. If you're moving a token such as USDC or USDT, first send a small amount of that network's own coin (ETH, SOL or TRX) to the address shown. This pays the network fee for the next step. For Tron, the screen suggests around 15 TRX.
2. Copy the key. It's labelled "Private key (WIF)", "Secret key" or "Private key", depending on the network.
3. Import the key into any crypto wallet that supports that network.
4. In that crypto wallet, send your crypto to an address you control.

## Move crypto on Polkadot

The screen is titled "Recover funds" and shows "Address on Polkadot" and a key labelled "Raw seed".

1. Copy the raw seed.
2. Import it as a raw seed into a Polkadot crypto wallet, such as Talisman (talisman.xyz).
3. In that crypto wallet, send your crypto to an address you control.

If a Send to yourself didn't finish, you may see a "Return funds" screen instead, with "Address on Asset Hub" and a "Private key". Follow the same steps.

> [!WARNING]
> Anyone who has this key can take the crypto at that address. Don't share it, and move your crypto as soon as you've imported the key.

If you see "Your recovery address and key can't be loaded on this device. Open this top-up in the Polkadot App to reach them.", open it from **Get** in the Polkadot app on your phone.

## Still need help?

Email us at [help@pcf.network](mailto:help@pcf.network), or in the app tap **Settings** > **Legal & Support** > **Contact us**. Tell us your app version (it's at the bottom of **Settings**) and the exact message you saw.

Include the "Reference" from the payment's screen as well. Never send us the key.
