You can add ᴄᴀꜱʜ with a card in supported countries. You pay on a payment provider's page inside the app. The payment provider will likely ask to check your identity documents and request information about you to facilitate the transaction. This is the [ID check](id-check.md).
Getting ᴄᴀꜱʜ with a payment card usually takes a few minutes. 


## Add ᴄᴀꜱʜ with a card

1. Tap **Pocket** in the dock, tap your ᴄᴀꜱʜ purse, then tap **Get**.
2. On "Top up funds", tap **Card**.
3. Type how much ᴄᴀꜱʜ you want, then tap **Continue**.
4. Check the "Top up via card" screen. It shows the total "Will be charged to the card inc. fees", your "Payment country", what "You'll receive" and when it "Arrives".
5. If the country is wrong, tap **Payment country** and choose yours.
6. Tap **Continue**. The provider's "Secure payment" page opens inside the app.
7. Follow the provider's steps to check your ID and pay.

After you pay, the app shows "Checking your payment", then adds your ᴄᴀꜱʜ. When it's done, you'll see the amount, for example "+$50 ᴄᴀꜱʜ".

If the payment provider is retrying, you'll see "Delayed, don't pay again". Wait for it to finish rather than paying a second time.

## Fees

You'll see every fee before you pay: "Provider fee", "Network fee", "Mint fee" and "Service fee", then "Total fees" and what "You'll pay". The "Rate" line shows what $1 ᴄᴀꜱʜ costs in your currency. Fees that are zero aren't listed.

## Who handles the payment

Card payments go through Meld, which picks a payment provider for your country, such as Transak. The provider (Transak, for example) takes your card details and performs the ID check. 

The app suggests a payment country based on your internet connection and your phone's language settings. You can change it if the auto-complete is incorrect. 

## If card isn't available where you are

You'll see "Card transfer isn't available in this region". Tap **Use crypto** or **Use bank instead**.
