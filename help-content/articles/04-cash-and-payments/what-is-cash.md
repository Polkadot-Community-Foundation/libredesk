Cᴀꜱʜ is the digital cash you hold and send in the Polkadot app. It's worth US dollars, so $1 ᴄᴀꜱʜ is worth $1. You send it to people in your chats, and sending or receiving it has no fees.

## How it works

Your ᴄᴀꜱʜ lives in the ᴄᴀꜱʜ purse in your Pocket. Holding it is free.

Your ᴄᴀꜱʜ is made of a handful of digital coins of different sizes, the way $37 in your pocket is made of several notes. When you pay someone, your app picks the coins that make the exact amount and hands them over inside your encrypted chat. Their app then makes those coins its own, like a note going into someone's pocket.

Every so often a coin needs refreshing before it can be spent again. While that happens, it shows as Clearing. See [Cᴀꜱʜ: What Ready and Clearing mean](ready-and-clearing.md).

No bank or company issues ᴄᴀꜱʜ, and there's no one to freeze your account.

## What this means for you

Every payment is private. It travels end-to-end encrypted through your chat, and nobody else can see who paid whom. Your payments aren't linked to your username, and other people can't see how much ᴄᴀꜱʜ you hold.

You can pay anyone you chat with. To pay someone new, start a chat with them first.

Adding ᴄᴀꜱʜ and cashing out go through other companies, and using a card or bank transfer for either needs an ID check. Once it's in, your ᴄᴀꜱʜ is private.

## Getting ᴄᴀꜱʜ in and out

- Add ᴄᴀꜱʜ with crypto, a card, or a bank transfer in supported countries. See [How to get ᴄᴀꜱʜ](../05-get-and-send-to-yourself/ways-to-get-cash.md).
- Move ᴄᴀꜱʜ out to a crypto wallet any time, or cash out to your bank in supported countries.

See [Send ᴄᴀꜱʜ to your crypto wallet](../05-get-and-send-to-yourself/send-to-yourself-crypto.md) and [Send ᴄᴀꜱʜ to your bank or card](../05-get-and-send-to-yourself/send-to-yourself-bank-or-card.md).
