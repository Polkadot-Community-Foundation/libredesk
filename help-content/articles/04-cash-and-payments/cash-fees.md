Sending ᴄᴀꜱʜ to people in your chats is free, and receiving or holding it is free too. 
Adding ᴄᴀꜱʜ and sending it to yourself out of the app uses other companies, and those have different fees, depending on the country and method you're using. The app always shows any third-party fees before you confirm.

| What you do                           | Fees                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Send ᴄᴀꜱʜ to someone in your chats    | Free                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Receive ᴄᴀꜱʜ                          | Free                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Hold ᴄᴀꜱʜ                             | Free                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Get ᴄᴀꜱʜ with crypto                  | Built into the amount the app asks you to send. There are no separate fee lines.                                                                                     |
| Get ᴄᴀꜱʜ with a card or bank transfer | Shown before you pay, as "Provider fee", "Network fee", "Mint fee" and "Service fee"                                                                                 |
| Send ᴄᴀꜱʜ to another crypto wallet    | Shown before you confirm, as "Network fee", plus "Swap fee" and "Service fee" for some networks                                                                      |
| Send ᴄᴀꜱʜ to your bank or card        | Shown before you confirm, as provider, network and service fees, plus swapping fees that vary <!-- REVIEW F04: confirm bank and card cash-out is live at launch. --> |

## Why sending is free

Sending ᴄᴀꜱʜ between people runs on a generous free tier that comes with device verification. The app tells you if you reach it.

## Limits

There are no limits for sending ᴄᴀꜱʜ to other users in the app.
