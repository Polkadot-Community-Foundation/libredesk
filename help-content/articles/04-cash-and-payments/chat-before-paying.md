You see this when you try to pay someone from **Send** in your Pocket, but you don't have a chat with them yet. Payments travel through your encrypted chat, so you can only pay people you already have a chat with. The app opens a chat with them so you can start one.

## What to do

1. In the chat that opens, write a message and send it. This is your message request, and you can send one message until they accept.
2. Wait for them to accept. Until they do, you'll see "Wait for jake.23 to accept your message request", with their username in place of jake.23.
3. When they accept, the chat shows "jake.23 approved your request".
4. Tap the **$** button next to the message box to pay them.

If you're paying someone for the first time, check their full username, including the two digits at the end.
