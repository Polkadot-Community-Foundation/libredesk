Your Pocket holds your ᴄᴀꜱʜ purse and your ID card. To open it, tap **Pocket**, the second icon from the left in the dock at the bottom of the screen.

## Your ᴄᴀꜱʜ purse

Tap the purse to open it. Inside you'll find:

- your Total, which is all the ᴄᴀꜱʜ you hold. If some of it isn't ready to spend yet, your Ready amount shows above it
- **Get**, to add ᴄᴀꜱʜ with crypto, a card or a bank transfer
- **Send**, to pay someone in your chats or send ᴄᴀꜱʜ to yourself
- your coins, grouped as Ready and Clearing

While the app checks your ᴄᴀꜱʜ, you'll see "Updating balance".



## Your ID card

Your ID card shows your username and a QR code. People can scan it to add you or pay you.
