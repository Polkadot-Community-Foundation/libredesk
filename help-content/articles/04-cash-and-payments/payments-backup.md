Payments backup shows whether the app has finished setting up your payments. During setup, the app records where your coins are, so it can find your ᴄᴀꜱʜ if you reinstall the app or restore your account. It runs on its own after you set up Polkadot.

You'll find it in **Settings**, under "Security & privacy". It only shows a status, so there's nothing to tap.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| "In progress" | Setup is running. You can keep using payments. |
| "Delayed" | Setup is taking longer than expected, and it carries on by itself. <!-- REVIEW F33: what causes "Delayed", and is there anything the person can do? --> |
| "Done" | Setup is finished. |

## "We're still setting up payments"

While setup is running, you may see "We're still setting up payments. You can keep using them, but settlement will be delayed until setup is finished."

You can send and receive ᴄᴀꜱʜ as usual. Payments may take longer to complete until setup finishes.

## What to do

Keep the app open with a working internet connection, and setup carries on. If Payments backup stays on "Delayed", contact us.

Payments backup is separate from your account backup. To check that your account itself is backed up, see [Backing up your Polkadot app with Google Drive](../06-backup-and-recovery/back-up-to-google-drive.md).

## Still need help?

Email us at [help@pcf.network](mailto:help@pcf.network), or in the app tap **Settings** > **Legal & Support** > **Contact us**. Tell us your app version (it's at the bottom of **Settings**) and the exact message you saw.
