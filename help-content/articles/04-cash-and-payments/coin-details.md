Each coin in your ᴄᴀꜱʜ purse has two visual details to help you understand your privacy. These don't track data, but are just visual indications.
* Prior owners - How many people have held the coin before you. 
* Coin Quality - How hard it is to tell the coin apart from other coins.

You don't need to do anything with either. It's just a nice visual representation. 

## See a coin's details

1. Tap **Pocket**, then your ᴄᴀꜱʜ purse.
2. On the "Total" card, tap the strip of coins. They spread out under the headings "Ready" and "Clearing".
3. Tap a coin to see its value, "Prior owners" and "Coin Quality".
4. To put it back, tap the coin again or tap an empty space.

You can't select coins that don't have details yet.

## Prior owners

Your ᴄᴀꜱʜ is made of digital coins, and each time a coin changes hands, it ages. "Prior owners: 3" means three people held this coin before it reached you. 

After a set number of hand-offs, the app refreshes the coin. It replaces the coin with many identical coins and gets back a fresh one with no history. While that happens, the coin shows as Clearing.

## Coin Quality

The app rates each coin "Very low", "Low", "Fair", "Good" or "High", or shows "Unknown". Tap the info icon next to it to see the app's description: "The degree to which the coin is indistinguishable from other coins".

The higher the quality, the harder it is to tell the coin apart from others like it.

Your ᴄᴀꜱʜ Privacy Mode decides how often your coins are refreshed. 
See [Choose a ᴄᴀꜱʜ Privacy Mode](privacy-mode.md).
