Cᴀꜱʜ Privacy Mode lets you choose between speed and extra privacy. Every payment is private. The mode decides how often your coins are refreshed, which changes how long ᴄᴀꜱʜ you receive spends Clearing before it's Ready.

## Change your mode

1. Tap **Settings** in the dock.
2. Under "Security & privacy", find ᴄᴀꜱʜ Privacy Mode.
3. Tap **Fastest**, **Balanced** or **Most Private**, or drag between them.

## The three modes

| Mode                  | On screen                          | What it does                                                                                                                      |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fastest (the default) | "Fastest payments, lower privacy"  | Refreshes coins only when it has to. Cᴀꜱʜ you receive is ready to spend straight away.                                            |
| Balanced              | "Stronger privacy, moderate speed" | Refreshes coins more often. You can spend early after a confirmation.                                                             |
| Most Private          | "Maximum privacy, lowest speed"    | Refreshes coins after every hand-off and waits for a large crowd of similar coins. You can't spend your ᴄᴀꜱʜ before it refreshes. |

Refreshing replaces a coin with many identical coins and gives you back a fresh one with no history. The longer you wait after receiving ᴄᴀꜱʜ before you spend it, the more private it is.

## Which one to choose

The Fastest setting suits people who receive and spend very frequently, because ᴄᴀꜱʜ is ready to spend the moment it arrives. 
For the majority of people who are privacy-conscious, the Balanced or Most Private setting is ideal, depending on how quickly you need to spend your ᴄᴀꜱʜ once you receive it. You can change your mode at any time.
