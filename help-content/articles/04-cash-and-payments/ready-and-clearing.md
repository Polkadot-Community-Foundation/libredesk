Ready is the ᴄᴀꜱʜ you can spend now without reducing your privacy. Clearing is everything else: ᴄᴀꜱʜ that's still arriving, and coins that are being refreshed to keep your payments private. Clearing ᴄᴀꜱʜ is still yours, and Ready and Clearing together make up your Total.

## Where you see them

Tap **Pocket**, then your ᴄᴀꜱʜ purse. When some of your ᴄᴀꜱʜ is Clearing, the purse shows your Ready amount above your Total. Your coins are grouped under "Ready" and "Clearing".

When you send ᴄᴀꜱʜ, the amount screen also shows how much you have Ready.

## Why ᴄᴀꜱʜ clears

Your ᴄᴀꜱʜ is made of digital coins. Each time a coin changes hands, it ages. After a set number of hand-offs, the app refreshes it. It replaces the coin with many identical coins and gets back a fresh one with no history.

While a coin is being refreshed, it shows as Clearing. This happens on its own, and you don't need to do anything.

## How long it takes

It depends on your ᴄᴀꜱʜ Privacy Mode.

| Mode | What to expect |
|---|---|
| Fastest (the default) | Cᴀꜱʜ you receive is Ready straight away. Coins are refreshed only when they have to be. |
| Balanced | Coins are refreshed more often, so ᴄᴀꜱʜ spends longer Clearing. You can spend some early after a confirmation. |
| Most Private | Coins are refreshed after every hand-off, and the app waits for a large crowd of similar coins. You can't spend Clearing ᴄᴀꜱʜ early. |

The longer you wait after receiving ᴄᴀꜱʜ before you spend it, the more private it is. To change your mode, see [Choose a ᴄᴀꜱʜ Privacy Mode](privacy-mode.md).

## Can I spend Clearing ᴄᴀꜱʜ?

Sometimes, depending on your mode. The amount screen may show a line such as "Extra $5 ᴄᴀꜱʜ is spendable, but at the risk of reducing your privacy". If you type more than your Ready amount, the app asks you to confirm "This payment might reduce your privacy". See [what that message means](payment-might-reduce-privacy.md).
