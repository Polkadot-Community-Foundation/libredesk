Ask the person to send it back. Payments can't be reversed once the other person has received them, and Polkadot support can't take ᴄᴀꜱʜ back for you. 

You can only pay people you have a chat with, so they're already in your chats and you can message them straight away.

## Check these first

1. Open the chat and look at the payment's status. If it says "Transaction failed" or "Invalid transaction", the payment didn't go through and the ᴄᴀꜱʜ wasn't sent. 
2. Check the username at the top of the chat, including the two digits at the end, so you know exactly who has it.

## If you know them

Send them a message saying what happened. To send it back, they tap the **$** button in your chat and send you the same amount.

## If you don't know them

Send a short, polite message asking them to return it.

If they ask for anything in return, such as a fee, your recovery phrase or a payment to "unlock" the ᴄᴀꜱʜ, it's a scam. Don't send anything else. If they keep messaging you, [block them](../03-chats-and-calls/block-someone.md).
