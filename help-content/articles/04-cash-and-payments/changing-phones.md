When you switch phones, it's important to restore your old account so you can access your ᴄᴀꜱʜ. After you restore your account on a phone, the app finds your ᴄᴀꜱʜ and asks you to check it. 
Tap **Update** until your Total looks right, then tap **Close**. The **Get** and **Send** buttons stay hidden until you've finished.

## Check your ᴄᴀꜱʜ

1. Tap **Pocket**, then your ᴄᴀꜱʜ purse.
2. You'll see "Balance restored" and "If it differs from the previous value, press Update until correct".
3. Compare your Total with the amount you had before.
4. If it's different, tap **Update**. While the app checks again, you'll see "Updating balance". Repeat until the Total is right. Given how ᴄᴀꜱʜ works, you sometimes need to do this several times.
5. When it's correct, tap **Close**.
6. The app asks "Done updating your balance?" and explains "Once you close this, manual update won't be available again". Tap **Yes, I'm done**.

The **Get** and **Send** buttons then appear in your purse.

## Why you need to do this

Your ᴄᴀꜱʜ is made of private coins, and the app has to find them again after a restore. Tap **Why update?** to see the app's explanation: "Private accounts don't support automatic syncing. Manually updating your balance helps keep it accurate."

## If the amount still looks wrong

Don't tap **Close** yet. Check that you're connected to the internet and tap **Update** a few more times. If the Total still isn't right, contact us before you close the check.
