You see this when you try to send more than your Ready ᴄᴀꜱʜ. 
To make up the amount, the app would use ᴄᴀꜱʜ that's still Clearing, which hasn't finished being refreshed. The payment still works, but it may be less private than usual.

You might also see a line on the amount screen before this, such as "Extra $5 ᴄᴀꜱʜ is spendable, but at the risk of reducing your privacy".

## What to do

To send the full amount now, tap the button that ends in "anyway", for example **Send $20 ᴄᴀꜱʜ anyway**.

To keep your privacy, tap **Cancel** and send no more than your Ready amount. You can send the rest once more of your ᴄᴀꜱʜ is Ready.

If you see "Amount exceeds your available balance. Enter a smaller amount." instead, you don't have enough ᴄᴀꜱʜ for that payment, even counting what's Clearing. Type a smaller amount.

In Most Private mode, the app doesn't let you spend Clearing ᴄᴀꜱʜ early, so you can only send what's Ready. 
See [Choose a ᴄᴀꜱʜ Privacy Mode](privacy-mode.md).
