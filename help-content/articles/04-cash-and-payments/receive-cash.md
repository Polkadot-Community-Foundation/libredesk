Anyone you have a chat with can send you ᴄᴀꜱʜ, and receiving it has no fees. 
Someone new needs to find you and start a chat with you first.

## Let people find you

- Tell them your username, for example jake.23.
- Show them your ID card. Tap **Pocket**, then your ID card, and let them scan the QR code.
- Send them your ID card. On the ID card, tap the share icon at the top right.

When they send you a message request, accept it. After that, they can pay you. 
See [How message requests work](../03-chats-and-calls/message-requests.md).

## When someone sends you ᴄᴀꜱʜ

1. If notifications are on, you get one saying they're sending you ᴄᴀꜱʜ.
2. In your chat with them, the payment shows their username and "is sending you", for example "jake.23 is sending you". Its status is "Receiving…".
3. When it arrives, the message changes to "jake.23 sent you" and the status changes to "Received". The ᴄᴀꜱʜ is now in your ᴄᴀꜱʜ purse.

Sometimes you might see a status like "Received $3 ᴄᴀꜱʜ, waiting for the rest…". That means part of the payment has arrived and the rest is still on its way.

## When you can spend it

If you use the default ᴄᴀꜱʜ Privacy Mode, Fastest, the ᴄᴀꜱʜ is Ready to spend the moment it arrives. In Balanced or Most Private mode, it shows as Clearing for longer first. 

See [Cᴀꜱʜ: What Ready and Clearing mean](ready-and-clearing.md).
