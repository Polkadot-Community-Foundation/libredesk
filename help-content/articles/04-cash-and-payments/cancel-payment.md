No. Once you tap **Send**, you can't cancel the payment, and nobody can reverse it once the other person has received it. That includes Polkadot support. 

If the other person's app doesn't confirm the payment in time, it doesn't go through and your ᴄᴀꜱʜ stays with you. You'll see "The recipient didn't confirm the payment in time." You can try again later.

## What you can do instead

- Ask them to send it back. They can tap the **$** button in your chat and send you the same amount.
- If you paid the wrong person, follow the steps in [I sent ᴄᴀꜱʜ to the wrong person](sent-to-wrong-person.md).
- If someone tricked you into paying, see [Scams to watch for](../07-safety-and-scams/common-scams.md).

Before you send, check the username at the top of the chat, including the number at the end.
