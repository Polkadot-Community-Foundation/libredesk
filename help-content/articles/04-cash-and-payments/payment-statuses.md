Each payment in a chat shows a status. "Sending…" and "Receiving…" mean it's on its way. "Sent" means it has left you, and "Received" means it has arrived.

## If you sent the payment

| Status | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "Sending…" | Your app is sending the payment. | Wait for it to change to "Sent". |
| "Sent" | The payment has left your ᴄᴀꜱʜ purse and is on its way to them. | Nothing. It changes to "Received" when it arrives. |
| "Received" | The other person has the ᴄᴀꜱʜ. | Nothing. |
| "Transaction failed" or "Invalid transaction" | The payment didn't go through. | Check your ᴄᴀꜱʜ purse, then try again. <!-- REVIEW F11: confirm the ᴄᴀꜱʜ stays with the sender when a payment shows either status. --> |

## If someone sent you a payment

| Status | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "jake.23 is sending you" with "Receiving…" | The payment is on its way to you. | Wait. |
| "Received $3 ᴄᴀꜱʜ, waiting for the rest…" | Part of the payment has arrived, and the rest is still on its way. | Wait. |
| "jake.23 sent you" with "Received" | The ᴄᴀꜱʜ is in your ᴄᴀꜱʜ purse. | Nothing. |
| "Amount received differs from amount sent" | You got a different amount from the one they sent. | Ask them to check the amount they sent. If it still doesn't match, contact us. |
| "Transaction failed" or "Invalid transaction" | The payment didn't reach you. | Ask them to send it again. |
