You can send ᴄᴀꜱʜ to anyone you already have a chat with. 
Do it from inside the chat or from your Pocket. As always, sending ᴄᴀꜱʜ has no fees.

## Send from a chat

1. Open the chat with the person you want to pay.
2. Tap the **$** button next to the message box.
3. Type the amount. Above the keypad you'll see how much of your ᴄᴀꜱʜ is Ready to send.
4. Tap **Send**. The button shows the amount, for example **Send $5 ᴄᴀꜱʜ**.

The payment appears in the chat. Its status changes from "Sending…" to "Sent", then to "Received" once it reaches them.


## Send from your Pocket

1. Tap **Pocket** in the dock, then tap your ᴄᴀꜱʜ purse.
2. Tap **Send**.
3. Type their username, tap **Paste**, or tap the scan icon to scan their ID card.
4. Choose them from the results.
5. Type the amount and tap **Send**.

When it's done, you'll see "Your transaction was sent successfully". Tap **Done**.

If you haven't chatted with them yet, the app says "You need to first establish a chat before sending a payment" and opens a chat for you. Once they accept your message request, you can pay them.

## What the other person sees

While the ᴄᴀꜱʜ is on its way, their chat shows your username followed by "is sending you", for example "jake.23 is sending you". Once it has arrived, it changes to "jake.23 sent you". If they use the default ᴄᴀꜱʜ Privacy Mode, they can spend it straight away.

## If you can't send the full amount

The amount you can send without reducing your privacy is shown as Ready. If you type more than that, the app may ask you to confirm with "This payment might reduce your privacy". See [what that message means](payment-might-reduce-privacy.md).

> [!INFO]
> Payments can't be reversed once the other person has received them. If you sent ᴄᴀꜱʜ by mistake, ask them to send it back.
