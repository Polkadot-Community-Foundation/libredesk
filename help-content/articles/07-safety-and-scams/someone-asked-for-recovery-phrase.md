Anyone who asks for your recovery phrase is trying to take your ᴄᴀꜱʜ. Don't share it, however the request is worded.

> [!WARNING]
> Anyone who has your recovery phrase can open your account and spend your ᴄᴀꜱʜ. Polkadot support will never ask for it.

## How it usually goes

The request rarely says "send me your recovery phrase". It usually looks like help:

- a message request from someone who says they're from Polkadot support and that your account needs "verifying" or "syncing"
- a link to a website that asks for your words to fix a payment or keep your account open
- someone offering to fix a problem or get ᴄᴀꜱʜ back for you, if you share the words first

## Signs to look for

- They ask for your words, a screenshot of them, or a photo of where you wrote them down.
- They're in a hurry, or say something bad will happen if you don't act now.
- They send a link where you're meant to type the words. The only place your recovery phrase belongs is the Polkadot app, when you restore your account.

## What to do now

1. Don't send them any of the words.
2. Block them: in the chat, tap **⋮** > **Block user**.
3. If you've already shared your recovery phrase, act straight away, because whoever has it can spend your ᴄᴀꜱʜ. 
4. Contact us at [help@pcf.network](mailto:help@pcf.network) and tell us what happened.
