Before you pay someone, check the full username at the top of the chat, including the two digits after the dot. Usernames look like jake.23, and anyone can pick the same name with different digits, such as jake.32.

## How it usually goes

A message request arrives from someone using a friend's name. They say they've lost their phone or started a new account, and ask you to send ᴄᴀꜱʜ quickly.

## Signs to look for

- It arrives as a message request, even though you already have a chat with that friend.
- The digits are different from the ones in your usual chat with them.
- They say they've changed their username. Usernames can't be changed.
- They're in a hurry, or ask you to keep it between the two of you.

A friend who restores their account on a new phone keeps the same username. A different username means a different account.

## What to do now

1. Compare the username with the one in your existing chat with your friend.
2. Check with your friend another way, such as a phone call or in person.
3. If it isn't them, decline the message request or block them. In the chat, tap **⋮** > **Block user**.

When you meet someone in person, the most reliable way to add them is to scan the QR code on their ID card. See [Use your ID card](../02-username-and-id/id-card.md).
