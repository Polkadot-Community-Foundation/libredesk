Your account lives on your phone, so keeping your phone safe keeps your account safe. Each of these steps takes a few minutes.

## Lock your phone

Set a screen lock, such as a PIN, pattern, password or fingerprint, in your phone's settings. The Polkadot app asks for it before it shows your recovery phrase. Anyone who can unlock your phone can open the app.

## Keep a backup

- On the Google Play version, check that **Settings** > **Backup** says "Account is backed up". See [Backing up your Polkadot app with Google Drive](../06-backup-and-recovery/back-up-to-google-drive.md).
- On the direct download version (for GrapheneOS and phones without Google Play), write down your recovery phrase and keep it somewhere only you can get to. See [Back up without Google Drive](../06-backup-and-recovery/back-up-without-google-drive.md).

If you have no backup, the Backup row in Settings says "Not backed up. Set up a backup to stay protected".

## Keep your recovery phrase to yourself

Anyone who has your recovery phrase can open your account and spend your ᴄᴀꜱʜ. Polkadot support will never ask for it. Don't type it into any website or other app.

## Get the app from the right place

Install the Google Play version from Google Play, and the direct download version only from [official download page]. Keep your phone and the app up to date. See [Update the app](../08-settings-and-account/update-the-app.md).

## Choose who can reach you

Strangers can send you one message request, and nothing more until you accept. Decline requests you don't want, and block anyone you don't want to hear from. See [How message requests work](../03-chats-and-calls/message-requests.md).

## Plan for a lost phone

Turn on your phone's find-my-device service (on most Android phones this is Google's Find Hub), so you can lock or erase your phone if you lose it. If you do lose your phone, change your Google password straight away, because your backup is in your Google Drive. See [I lost my phone](../06-backup-and-recovery/lost-phone.md).
