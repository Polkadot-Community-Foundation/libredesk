Polkadot support only uses email, and only from help@pcf.network. We'll never ask for your recovery phrase or ask you to send ᴄᴀꜱʜ. Anyone who does is trying to take your ᴄᴀꜱʜ, even if they use the Polkadot name or logo.

## How to reach us

- Email [help@pcf.network](mailto:help@pcf.network) from any phone or computer.
- Or in the app, tap **Settings** > **Legal & Support** > **Contact us**. This opens your email app with our address filled in.

## How we contact you

We reply by email from help@pcf.network. Anyone else who says they're Polkadot support isn't us. That includes message requests in the app, messages on social media and emails from any other address.

## What we'll never do

- Ask for your recovery phrase, or ask you to type it into a website or another app.
- Ask you to send ᴄᴀꜱʜ to check, protect or unlock your account.
- Charge a fee to get your account or your ᴄᴀꜱʜ back. Nobody can open your account without your recovery phrase or backup, and that includes us.
- Ask you to install another app or share your screen.

## If someone says they're from Polkadot support

1. Don't share your recovery phrase, and don't send any ᴄᴀꜱʜ.
2. Stop replying. If they messaged you in the app, block them: in the chat, tap **⋮** > **Block user**.
3. Email [help@pcf.network](mailto:help@pcf.network) yourself and ask whether the message was really from us.
