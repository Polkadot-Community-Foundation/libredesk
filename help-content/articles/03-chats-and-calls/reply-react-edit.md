Long-press any message to reply to it, react with an emoji or copy it. You can also edit messages you've sent.

## Reply to a message

Swipe the message to the left, or long-press it and tap **Reply**. A "Reply to" banner appears above the message box. Type your reply and send it.

## React with an emoji

1. Long-press the message.
2. Tap an emoji in the row that appears. For more choices, tap the expand button at the end of the row.

To remove your reaction, tap it under the message.

## Edit a message

You can edit your own messages, and the captions on photos and videos you've sent.

1. Long-press your message and tap **Edit**.
2. Change the text and send it.

To stop editing without changing anything, tap the **X** on the "Edit" banner. Once you've changed a message, it shows "Edited". The other person sees "Edited" too, and can open **Edit history**, so editing doesn't hide what you first wrote.

## See earlier versions of a message

Long-press a message marked "Edited" and tap **Edit history**. You'll see each version and when it was changed.

## Copy a message

Long-press the message and tap **Copy**.
