Every message is end-to-end encrypted, so only you and the person you're talking to can read it. Photos and videos are encrypted before they leave your phone, and voice and video calls are encrypted and peer-to-peer.

## How it works

- Messages are end-to-end encrypted on your phone before they're sent. They're delivered over Polkadot's public network, rather than a company server.
- Photos and videos are encrypted on your phone, and only the encrypted file is uploaded for delivery.
- Voice and video calls are encrypted and peer-to-peer. A service run by the Polkadot Community Foundation helps users to connect, but doesn't have any logging or analyitcs enabled.  
- You don't need a phone number or email. People find you by your username.
- Strangers can send you one message request, and nothing more until you accept. See [How message requests work](message-requests.md).

If you'd like the technical detail, see [how encryption works](TODO-developer-docs-url).

## What this means for you

- Your messages are stored on your phone. Anyone who can unlock your phone can open the app and read them, so use a screen lock.
- The person you're chatting with can still copy or screenshot anything you send.
- The app occasionally can't send a message privately right away. When that happens it asks before using your username. See ["This chat can't be fully private right now"](chat-not-fully-private.md).
