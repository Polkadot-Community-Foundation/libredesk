Find the person by their username, or scan the QR code on their ID card. Your first message reaches them as a message request. Once they accept, you can chat, call and send ᴄᴀꜱʜ to each other.

## Start a chat by username

1. Swipe up on the scan button in the middle of the dock. You can also tap **+** at the top right of **Chats**.
2. Type their username in the box that says "Type username".
3. Tap their name in the results. The chat opens.
4. Write your first message and send it.

Above the message box you'll see, for example, "Invite jake.23 to chat" and "You can only send one message in this request." After you send it, you'll see "Request message sent", and the message box changes to "Wait for jake.23 to accept your message request."

## Start a chat by scanning a QR code

This works well when you're with the person.

1. Ask them to open **Pocket** and tap their ID card, so their QR code shows on screen.
2. Tap the scan button in the middle of your dock.
3. Point your camera at their QR code. Their chat opens.
4. Write your first message and send it.

If the camera area says "Allow camera access to scan QR codes", tap it and allow access. If you see "Invalid QR code", check that you're scanning their Polkadot ID card and not your own.

## Once they accept

When they accept, you'll see "jake.23 approved your request" in the chat. From then on, you can message, call and send ᴄᴀꜱʜ to each other.

## If the chat won't start

If you see "Couldn't start a chat with this user. Ask them to open the app to finish setting up chats.", ask them to open Polkadot, then try again.
