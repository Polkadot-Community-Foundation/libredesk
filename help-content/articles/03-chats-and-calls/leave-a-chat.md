Leaving a chat deletes it, and the contact, from your phone. The other person sees a message saying you left the chat.

## Leave a chat

1. Open the chat.
2. Tap **⋮** at the top right.
3. Tap **Leave chat**.
4. You'll see "Leave chat?". Tap **Delete** to confirm.

**Leave chat** shows once your chat with them is set up, or after they've left.

## What happens when you leave

- The messages and the contact are deleted from your phone. This can't be undone.
- The other person sees, for example, "jake.23 left the chat" (with your username).
- Nothing is removed from their phone.

To talk to them again later, start a new chat. See [Start a chat](start-a-chat.md).

If you want them to stop contacting you, block them instead. See [Block or unblock someone](block-someone.md).
