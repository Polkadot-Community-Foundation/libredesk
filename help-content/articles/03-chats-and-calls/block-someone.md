Blocking someone stops them sending you message requests or calling you. You can unblock them at any time.

## Block someone

1. Open the chat with them.
2. Tap **⋮** at the top right.
3. Tap **Block user**.
4. Tap **Block** to confirm.

You go back to your chat list. You won't get messages, message requests or calls from them while they're blocked.

**Block user** shows once your chat with them is set up. If they've only sent you a message request, tap **Decline** instead. See [How message requests work](message-requests.md).

## Unblock someone

You can unblock someone from their chat or from Settings:

- Open their chat and tap **Unblock**. While someone is blocked, their chat says "You won't receive any messages and requests until you unblock" followed by their username.
- Or tap **Settings** > **Blocked contacts**, then tap **Unblock** next to their name.
