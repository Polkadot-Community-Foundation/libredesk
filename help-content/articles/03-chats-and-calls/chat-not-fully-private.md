You see this when the app can't send your message in its usual private way. Normally the app sends chat messages without linking them to your username, and to do that it needs a free private slot. When they're all taken, it asks you what to do. <!-- REVIEW F08: check this plain-language explanation of private slots with the product team. -->

The full message reads: "All private slots are taken. You can send with your username instead: the chat stays end-to-end encrypted, but some activity, like when you send messages, may be linked to your username. If you don't, waiting messages won't be sent."

## What to do

Choose one of the two buttons:

- **Use username** sends your messages now. The chat is still end-to-end encrypted, so nobody else can read what you write. Some activity, like when you send messages, may be linked to your username.
- **Don't send** holds your waiting messages, and they aren't sent. The next time you send a message in this chat, the app asks again.

If you choose **Use username**, a small broken-shield icon appears next to their name at the top of the chat. Tap it to see "This chat isn't fully private", then tap **Got it**.
