You can make encrypted, peer-to-peer voice and video calls to anyone you have a chat with. Start a call from the top right of the chat.

## Start a call

1. Open the chat with the person you want to call.
2. Tap the phone icon for a voice call, or the camera icon for a video call. They're at the top right of the chat.
3. If the app asks, allow access to your microphone, and to your camera for a video call.

You'll see "Contacting…", then "Ringing…" while their phone rings, then "Connecting…" as the call starts.

The call icons appear once the chat is set up, which means after they've accepted your message request or you've accepted theirs.

## Answer a call

When someone calls you, you'll see "Incoming call". Tap **accept** to answer, or **end** to decline.

## During a call

- **speaker** chooses where the sound plays: **Phone**, **Speaker**, **Headphones** or **Bluetooth**.
- **video** turns your camera on or off.
- **mute** turns your microphone off. You'll see "Your microphone is off".
- **end** hangs up.

You can be in one call at a time. 

## Calls in your chat

Each call shows in the chat, for example "Missed voice call" or "Video call". Tap one to call back. If you're still in a call, tap the entry that says "Tap to return" to go back to it.

## If you use the direct download version

The direct download version (for GrapheneOS and phones without Google Play) doesn't get push notifications, so you may only see incoming calls while the app is open. <!-- REVIEW F13: confirm whether incoming calls ring when the app isn't running on the direct download version. -->
