When someone you haven't chatted with writes to you, their first message arrives as a message request. They can send one message until you accept.

## Find your message requests

When you have requests waiting, a "New requests" row appears at the top of **Chats**, with a count. Tap it to open "Message requests".

## Accept a request

1. Tap the request to read the message.
2. Tap **Accept**.

They won't know you've seen their message until you accept. Once you do, they see that you approved their request, and you can both message, call and send ᴄᴀꜱʜ.

## Decline a request

- From the list, tap **Decline** next to the request, then tap **Decline** again to confirm.
- Or open the request and tap **Decline**.

The person who sent it isn't told that you declined. Their chat keeps showing that they're waiting for you to accept. 

## When you send a request

Your first message to someone new is a message request too. After you send it, you'll see "Wait for (username) to accept your message request." (with their username). You can send your next message once they accept.

If they decline, you won't be told.
