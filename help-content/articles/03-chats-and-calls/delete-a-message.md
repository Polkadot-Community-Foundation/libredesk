No. Once you've sent a message, you can't delete it, from your phone or from the other person's.

## What you can do instead

You can edit a message you've sent: long-press it and tap **Edit**. The other person sees "Edited" on it and can open **Edit history**, so editing doesn't hide what you first wrote. 
See [Reply to, react to and edit messages](reply-react-edit.md).

You can leave the chat. This deletes the whole chat from your phone, but not from theirs. See [Leave a chat](leave-a-chat.md).

If you don't want to hear from someone again, block them. See [Block or unblock someone](block-someone.md).
