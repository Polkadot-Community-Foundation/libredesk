You can send a photo or video from your gallery, or take a new photo with your camera. Photos and videos are encrypted before they leave your phone.

## Send a photo or video

1. Open the chat.
2. If you want to add a caption, type it in the message box first.
3. Tap **+** next to the message box.
4. If the app asks for camera access, allow it if you want to take a new photo.
5. Choose your camera to take a photo, or a gallery app to pick a photo or video.
6. Pick one photo or video.

It's sent with any text you typed as its caption. While it uploads, you'll see "Uploading" with a percentage.

## What you can send

- Photos and videos, one at a time.
- Each file can be up to 128 MB. If it's bigger, you'll see "This file is too large to send. Choose one under 128 MB."
- Photos are compressed before they're sent, so they take up less space.

If an upload stops, you'll see "Uploading failed · retry". Tap it to try again.
