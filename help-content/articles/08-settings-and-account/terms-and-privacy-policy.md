You can read Polkadot's Terms of use and Privacy policy at any time. You agreed to them when you set up the app, where the screen says "By continuing you agree to our Terms and Privacy Policy".

## In the app

1. Tap **Settings** in the dock.
2. Tap **Legal & Support**.
3. Tap **Privacy policy** or **Terms of use**.
