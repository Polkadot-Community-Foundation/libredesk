Polkadot has five themes that change the look of the whole app: Berlin Night (dark), Berlin Day, Lisbon, Malta and Tokyo. You picked one when you set up, and you can change it at any time. 

## Change your theme

1. Tap **Settings** in the dock.
2. Tap **Theme**. The row shows the theme you're using now.
3. Tap a colour to choose a theme.

The new theme applies straight away, so there's nothing to save.
