No. There's no option in the app to delete your account or sign out. Your account lives on your phone, and your username is registered on Polkadot, a public network. 

## What you can do instead

1. Move your ᴄᴀꜱʜ out first. Use Send to yourself to move it to a crypto wallet or your bank. Any ᴄᴀꜱʜ you leave stays with the account. See [Send ᴄᴀꜱʜ to your crypto wallet](../05-get-and-send-to-yourself/send-to-yourself-crypto.md).
2. Uninstall the app. This removes the account from that phone.
3. If you use the Google Play version, remove your Polkadot backup from Google Drive: [steps to remove the backup]. 

If you've moved all your ᴄᴀꜱʜ out and won't come back, you can also destroy your written recovery phrase. Without it, or a backup, nobody can open the account again, including you.

## What stays after you uninstall

- Your username stays registered on Polkadot. 
- Anyone who has your recovery phrase or your Google Drive backup can restore the account, along with any ᴄᴀꜱʜ left in it.
- People you chatted with keep their own copies of your chats.
