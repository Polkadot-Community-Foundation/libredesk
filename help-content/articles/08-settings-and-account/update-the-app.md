If you installed Polkadot from Google Play, update it from the Play Store, like any other app. If you have the direct download version (for GrapheneOS and phones without Google Play), [how updates are delivered].

## Google Play version

1. Open the **Play Store** app.
2. Search for Polkadot.
3. If you see **Update**, tap it.

You can also turn on automatic updates in the Play Store, so Polkadot updates itself.

## When you need to update

If a message in a chat shows "Unsupported message content. Please update the app.", the other person has a newer version of the app. Update yours to see it.

To check your app version number, see [Find your app version](find-app-version.md).
