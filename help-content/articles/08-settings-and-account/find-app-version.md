Tap **Settings** in the dock and scroll to the bottom. Your app version is under the Polkadot logo, written as "App Version:" followed by the version and a build number in brackets.

Support may ask for this number, and for your Android version. To find your Android version, open your phone's **Settings** app and tap **About phone**. The exact menu names depend on your phone.
