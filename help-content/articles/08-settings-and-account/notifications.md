Polkadot uses your phone's own notification settings. Tap **Settings** > **Notifications** to open them, then turn each type on or off.

## Change your notifications

1. Tap **Settings** in the dock.
2. Tap **Notifications**. Your phone's notification settings for Polkadot open.
3. Turn notifications on or off for the whole app, or for each type:
   - "Chat messages": new messages in your chats
   - "Calls": incoming voice and video calls
   - "Background service workers": short notices while the app finishes something, such as "Sending attachment…"

If you said no when your phone first asked about notifications, you can turn them on here.

## Direct download version

The direct download version (for GrapheneOS and phones without Google Play) doesn't get push notifications, so new messages and calls only show up while Polkadot is open.
