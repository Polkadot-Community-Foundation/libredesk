Find the message you saw in the tables below, listed A to Z. Each one says what the message means and what to do next. Messages on the Get ᴄᴀꜱʜ and Send to yourself screens are covered in [I added ᴄᴀꜱʜ but it hasn't arrived](../05-get-and-send-to-yourself/added-cash-not-arrived.md) and [Send to yourself hasn't arrived or didn't work](../05-get-and-send-to-yourself/send-to-yourself-not-arrived.md).

## A to D

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "Amount exceeds your available balance. Enter a smaller amount." | You tried to send more ᴄᴀꜱʜ than you have. | Enter a smaller amount. See [How to send ᴄᴀꜱʜ](../04-cash-and-payments/send-cash.md). |
| "Amount received differs from amount sent" | The ᴄᴀꜱʜ that arrived doesn't match the amount in the payment. | Check with the other person. If it doesn't sort itself out, contact support. See [What the payment statuses mean](../04-cash-and-payments/payment-statuses.md). |
| "App safety check failed" | The app couldn't confirm it's safe to use on this phone. | See ["We couldn't verify this device"](../02-username-and-id/device-cant-be-verified.md). |
| "Connection lost" | A call dropped. | Check your connection and call again. See [Calls won't connect](calls-not-connecting.md). |
| "Couldn't check this username. Try again." | The app couldn't check whether the username is free. | Check your connection and try again. See [Choose your username](../02-username-and-id/choose-a-username.md). |
| "Couldn't check your balance. Try again." | The app couldn't check your ᴄᴀꜱʜ before sending. | Check your connection and try again. |
| "Couldn't confirm it's you. Try again." | Your screen lock check didn't go through. | Try again with your PIN, pattern, password or fingerprint. See [View your recovery phrase](../06-backup-and-recovery/view-recovery-phrase.md). |
| "Couldn't load search results. Please try again." | Search didn't load. | Check your connection and search again. |
| "Couldn't load this product." | The Get ᴄᴀꜱʜ or Send to yourself screen didn't open. | Check your connection and try again. |
| "Couldn't share your ID. Try again." | Your ID card couldn't be shared. | Try again. See [Use your ID card](../02-username-and-id/id-card.md). |
| "Couldn't sign the transaction. Try again." | The app couldn't finish an action on your phone. | Try again. If it keeps happening, contact support. |
| "Couldn't start a chat with this user. Ask them to open the app to finish setting up chats." | The other person's app hasn't finished setting up chats. | Ask them to open Polkadot, then try again. See [My messages aren't arriving](messages-not-arriving.md). |
| "Device verification is temporarily unavailable. Try again in a moment." | Device verification is busy. | Wait a moment and try again. |
| "Device verification isn't available" | Your phone doesn't support the device verification Polkadot uses. | See ["We couldn't verify this device"](../02-username-and-id/device-cant-be-verified.md). |
| "Digits taken. Try again." | Someone already has that username with that number. | Try again. See [Choose your username](../02-username-and-id/choose-a-username.md). |
| "Downloading failed · retry" | A photo or video didn't finish downloading. | Tap it to try again. See [Photos or videos won't send or download](photos-videos-not-sending.md). |

## G to P

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "Get ᴄᴀꜱʜ isn't available right now. Try again later." | Adding ᴄᴀꜱʜ isn't working at the moment. | Try again later. See [How to get ᴄᴀꜱʜ](../05-get-and-send-to-yourself/ways-to-get-cash.md). |
| "Google Play services aren't available on this device, so it can't be verified. Contact support for a build that works without them." | You have the Google Play version on a phone without Google Play services. | Use the direct download version. See [Google Play or direct download](../01-getting-started/choose-version.md). |
| "Invalid QR code" | The code you scanned isn't a Polkadot ID card, or it's your own. | Scan the other person's ID card. On the send screen, the app also says "Be sure you're scanning a Polkadot account address". |
| "Invalid recovery phrase" | The words you typed aren't a valid recovery phrase. | See ["Invalid recovery phrase"](../06-backup-and-recovery/invalid-recovery-phrase.md). |
| "Invalid transaction" | A payment in your chat didn't go through. | See [What the payment statuses mean](../04-cash-and-payments/payment-statuses.md). |
| "No App found" | **Contact us** needs an email app, and your phone doesn't have one set up. | Email [help@pcf.network](mailto:help@pcf.network) from any device. See [Contact support](contact-support.md). |
| "No backup was found in Google Drive. Recover with your recovery phrase instead." | There's no Polkadot backup in the Google account you chose. | Check you picked the right Google account, or use your recovery phrase. See [Restore your account on a new phone](../06-backup-and-recovery/restore-your-account.md). |
| "No connection. Check your network and try again." | Your phone isn't online. | See ["Waiting for network connection…" or "No connection"](no-connection.md). |
| "Our servers are having trouble. Try again in a few minutes." | The problem is on our side, not your phone. | Wait a few minutes and try again. |
| "Payment failed" | A payment from your Pocket didn't go through. | Check the amount and the person, then try again. See [What the payment statuses mean](../04-cash-and-payments/payment-statuses.md). |
| "Permissions denied" | Polkadot doesn't have a permission it needs for this screen, such as the camera or microphone. | Tap **Open Settings** and allow the permission. |

## S to T

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "Search failed. Please try again" | Searching your chats didn't work. | Check your connection and try again. |
| "Some messages could not be retrieved and are permanently unavailable." | Some messages couldn't be delivered and can't be recovered. | Ask the other person to send them again. See [My messages aren't arriving](messages-not-arriving.md). |
| "Taken. Try another." | Someone already has that username. | Try a different name. See [Choose your username](../02-username-and-id/choose-a-username.md). |
| "That didn't work. Try again — if it keeps happening, contact support." | Something didn't go through. | Try again. If it keeps happening, see [Contact support](contact-support.md). |
| "The recipient didn't confirm the payment in time. Your money is still yours — try again later." | The payment didn't go through, and your ᴄᴀꜱʜ stays with you. | Try again later. See [Can I cancel a payment?](../04-cash-and-payments/cancel-payment.md). |
| "This backup is damaged and can't be read. Recover with your recovery phrase instead." | Your Google Drive backup can't be used. | Restore with your recovery phrase. See [Restore your account on a new phone](../06-backup-and-recovery/restore-your-account.md). |
| "This chat can't be fully private right now" | The app needs your OK to send with your username. | See ["This chat can't be fully private right now"](../03-chats-and-calls/chat-not-fully-private.md). |
| "This contact is no longer available. It may have been removed." | The contact isn't in your app any more. | Search for their username to start a new chat. See [Start a chat](../03-chats-and-calls/start-a-chat.md). |
| "This file is too large to send. Choose one under 128 MB." | The photo or video is bigger than 128 MB. | Choose a smaller file. See [Send photos and videos](../03-chats-and-calls/send-photos-videos.md). |
| "This is taking longer than usual" | Something in the app is slower than normal. | See ["This is taking longer than usual"](taking-longer-than-usual.md). |
| "This payment might reduce your privacy" | You're sending ᴄᴀꜱʜ that isn't Ready yet. | See [Spending ᴄᴀꜱʜ that's Clearing](../04-cash-and-payments/payment-might-reduce-privacy.md). |
| "This username was just claimed. Try another." | Someone took that username while you were choosing it. | Try a different name. If the app says "We picked a new number for you", you can keep the new number. See [Choose your username](../02-username-and-id/choose-a-username.md). |
| "Those words don't match your recovery phrase. Try again." | The words you typed don't match this account's recovery phrase. | Check each word and the order. See ["Invalid recovery phrase"](../06-backup-and-recovery/invalid-recovery-phrase.md). |
| "Transaction failed" | A payment in your chat didn't go through. | See [What the payment statuses mean](../04-cash-and-payments/payment-statuses.md). |
| "Try another." | That username can't be used. | Try a different name. See [Choose your username](../02-username-and-id/choose-a-username.md). |

## U to Y

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "Unsupported message content. Please update the app." | Someone sent something that needs a newer version of the app. | Update Polkadot. See [Update the app](../08-settings-and-account/update-the-app.md). |
| "Uploading failed · retry" | A photo or video didn't finish sending. | Tap it to try again. See [Photos or videos won't send or download](photos-videos-not-sending.md). |
| "Waiting for network connection…" | The app is starting and can't get online. | See ["Waiting for network connection…" or "No connection"](no-connection.md). |
| "We couldn't verify this device. Rooted devices and custom ROMs aren't supported." | Your phone didn't pass device verification. | See ["We couldn't verify this device"](../02-username-and-id/device-cant-be-verified.md). |
| "Withdrawing ᴄᴀꜱʜ isn't available right now. Try again later." | Send to yourself isn't working at the moment. | Try again later. See [Send to yourself hasn't arrived or didn't work](../05-get-and-send-to-yourself/send-to-yourself-not-arrived.md). |
| "You don't have enough funds" | The amount is more than the ᴄᴀꜱʜ you have. | Enter a smaller amount, or [get more ᴄᴀꜱʜ](../05-get-and-send-to-yourself/ways-to-get-cash.md). |
| "You need to first establish a chat before sending a payment" | You can only pay people you already have a chat with. | See ["You need to first establish a chat before sending a payment"](../04-cash-and-payments/chat-before-paying.md). |
| "You won't receive any messages and requests until you unblock" | You've blocked this person. | Tap **Unblock** if you want to hear from them. See [Block or unblock someone](../03-chats-and-calls/block-someone.md). |
| "You're already in a call in another chat. End it before starting a new one." | You can be in one call at a time. | End your other call first. See [Make voice and video calls](../03-chats-and-calls/voice-video-calls.md). |

## Still need help?

Email us at [help@pcf.network](mailto:help@pcf.network), or in the app tap **Settings** > **Legal & Support** > **Contact us**. Tell us your app version (it's at the bottom of **Settings**) and the exact message you saw.
