You see this when something in the app, such as sending a payment, is taking longer than it normally does. It usually finishes on its own.

## What to do

1. Tap **Details** to see "What's happening". It shows the step the app is on, for example "Sending your payment", "Delivering payment to recipient" or "Waiting for the recipient to receive the payment".
2. Check your internet connection, and tap **Network Status** in the dock to see if every connection says "Connected".
3. Keep the app open and give it a few more minutes.
4. If it was a payment, check its status in the chat before you send it again. See [What the payment statuses mean](../04-cash-and-payments/payment-statuses.md).

Some steps in **Details** use technical names. You don't need to understand them, but they're useful to us if you contact support.

## If you were adding ᴄᴀꜱʜ or using Send to yourself

Those screens show their own version of this message, and they carry on automatically. See [I added ᴄᴀꜱʜ but it hasn't arrived](../05-get-and-send-to-yourself/added-cash-not-arrived.md).
