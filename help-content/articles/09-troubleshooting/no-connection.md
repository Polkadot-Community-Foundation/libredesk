Polkadot can't reach the internet from your phone. Most of the time, turning off Airplane Mode or switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data fixes it.

You might see one of these messages:

- "Waiting for network connection…" with "Turn off Airplane Mode or check your internet", when the app is starting
- "No connection. Check your network and try again."
- "No internet" in the Network Status panel

## Check these first

1. Make sure Airplane Mode is off.
2. Check that Wi-Fi or mobile data is on, and open a website in your browser to see if it loads.
3. Switch from Wi-Fi to mobile data, or the other way round.
4. If you use a VPN, try turning it off for a moment.
5. Close Polkadot completely and open it again.

To see which connections are working, tap **Network Status** at the far right of the dock. See [What the Network Status rings mean](network-status.md).

## If the app says "Our servers are having trouble"

The full message is "Our servers are having trouble. Try again in a few minutes." Your connection is fine, and the problem is on our side. Wait a few minutes and try again.

Email us at [help@pcf.network](mailto:help@pcf.network), or in the app tap **Settings** > **Legal & Support** > **Contact us**. Tell us your app version (it's at the bottom of **Settings**) and the exact message you saw.
