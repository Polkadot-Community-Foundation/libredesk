On the Google Play version, the usual cause is that notifications are turned off in your phone's settings for Polkadot. The direct download version doesn't get push notifications, so new messages and calls only show up while Polkadot is open.

## If you have the Google Play version

1. In Polkadot, tap **Settings** > **Notifications**. This opens your phone's notification settings for Polkadot.
2. Make sure notifications are allowed, and that "Chat messages" and "Calls" are both on.
3. Check that Do Not Disturb is off, or that it lets Polkadot through.
4. Open your phone's **Settings** > **Apps** > **Polkadot** > **Battery**, and make sure the app isn't restricted. Some phones stop apps running in the background to save battery.
5. Make sure Google Play services is installed and up to date. The Google Play version needs it for notifications.

## If you have the direct download version

The direct download version (for GrapheneOS and phones without Google Play) doesn't get push notifications. Open Polkadot to check for new messages and calls.

Not sure which one you have? See [Google Play or direct download: which version is for you?](../01-getting-started/choose-version.md)
