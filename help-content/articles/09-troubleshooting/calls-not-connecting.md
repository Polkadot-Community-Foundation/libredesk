Most call problems come from the chat not being set up yet, the app not having microphone access, or a weak connection on one side.

## Check these first

1. No call icons in the chat? They appear once the chat is set up, which means after they've accepted your message request or you've accepted theirs. See [How message requests work](../03-chats-and-calls/message-requests.md).
2. Check microphone and camera access. If you didn't allow it when asked, open your phone's **Settings** > **Apps** > **Polkadot** > **Permissions**, and allow **Microphone**, plus **Camera** for video calls. If the app shows "Permissions denied", tap **Open Settings** to go straight there.
3. Check your connection. Tap **Network Status** in the dock and check every row says "Connected". Try switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data.
4. Check you're not in another call. If you see "You're already in a call in another chat. End it before starting a new one.", end that call first.

## If the call stays on "Connecting…" or ends with "Connection lost"

The connection between the two phones dropped or couldn't be made. Ask the other person to check their connection too, then try again. You can tap the call entry in the chat ("Tap to call again") to retry.

## If they don't see your call

Their phone may not have shown a notification. Ask them to open Polkadot, then call again.

The direct download version (for GrapheneOS and phones without Google Play) doesn't get push notifications, so people using it may only see calls while the app is open. 

## Still need help?

Email us at [help@pcf.network](mailto:help@pcf.network), or in the app tap **Settings** > **Legal & Support** > **Contact us**. Tell us your app version (it's at the bottom of **Settings**) and the exact message you saw.
