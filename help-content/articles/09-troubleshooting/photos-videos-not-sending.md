Tap the photo or video to try again. If it still doesn't work, check your connection and the file size.

## If you see "Uploading failed · retry" or "Downloading failed · retry"

1. Check your internet connection. Tap **Network Status** in the dock to see if every row says "Connected".
2. Tap the message to retry.
3. If it stops again, switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data and retry.

While a photo or video is uploading, you'll see "Uploading" with a percentage, and a "Sending attachment…" notification. Large videos take longer, especially on mobile data.

## If you see "This file is too large to send"

Each photo or video can be up to 128 MB. Choose a smaller file, or a shorter video.

## If you can't pick the file you want

You can send photos and videos, one at a time. The picker only shows those. To take a new photo, allow Polkadot to use your camera when it asks. If you said no before, open your phone's **Settings** > **Apps** > **Polkadot** > **Permissions** and allow **Camera**.
