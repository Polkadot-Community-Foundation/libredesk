Email us at [help@pcf.network](mailto:help@pcf.network). If you have the app, you can also tap **Settings** > **Legal & Support** > **Contact us**, which opens a new email to us in your email app.

## Contact us from the app

1. Tap **Settings** in the dock.
2. Tap **Legal & Support**.
3. Tap **Contact us**.
4. Choose your email app, write your message and send it.

If you see "No App found", your phone doesn't have an email app set up. Email [help@pcf.network](mailto:help@pcf.network) from another phone or a computer instead.

## If you don't have your phone

Email [help@pcf.network](mailto:help@pcf.network) from another phone or a computer. Tell us your username and what happened.

## What to include

Tell us:

- your app version, which is at the bottom of **Settings** (see [Find your app version](../08-settings-and-account/find-app-version.md))
- whether you have the Google Play version or the direct download version
- your phone model and Android version
- the exact message you saw, and roughly when
- for adding ᴄᴀꜱʜ or Send to yourself, the "Reference" or "Transaction ID" from the details screen

> [!WARNING]
> Never send us your recovery phrase. Anyone who has your recovery phrase can open your account and spend your ᴄᴀꜱʜ. Polkadot support will never ask for it.
