To back up without Google Drive, write down your recovery phrase. On the direct download version (for GrapheneOS and phones without Google Play), this is the only way to restore your account on another phone.  
It works on the Google Play version too, if you'd rather not use Google Drive.

> [!WARNING]
> Anyone who has your recovery phrase can open your account and spend your ᴄᴀꜱʜ. 
> Polkadot support will never ask for it. Never give this information to anyone else.

## Write down your recovery phrase

1. Tap **Settings** > **Backup** > **View recovery phrase**.
2. Tap **Show recovery phrase**, unlock with your screen lock, then tap **Tap to reveal**.
3. Write each word on paper, numbered, in the order shown.
4. Check every word against the screen, including the spelling.

For more detail, see [View your recovery phrase](view-recovery-phrase.md).

## Keep it somewhere safe

- Store the information somewhere only you can get to. A second copy in another place helps if one gets lost or damaged.
- Don't photograph it, screenshot it, or keep it in notes, email, chats or cloud storage.
- Don't type it into any website or other app.


## What you'll see in Settings

On the direct download version, **Settings** may keep showing "Not backed up. Set up a backup to stay protected", because that status is about Google Drive. Once your recovery phrase is recorded, you can restore your account on another phone.
