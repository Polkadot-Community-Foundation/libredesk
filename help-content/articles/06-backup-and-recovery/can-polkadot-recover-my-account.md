No. Polkadot can't recover your account, reset it or tell you your recovery phrase. 
Your account lives on your phone, and only you hold the keys. 
The way back in is your Google Drive backup or your recovery phrase.

## What you can do instead

- If you still have your phone, back up now. See [Backing up your Polkadot app with Google Drive](back-up-to-google-drive.md) or [Back up without Google Drive](back-up-without-google-drive.md).
- If you've lost your phone, restore from your Google Drive backup or your recovery phrase. See [Restore your account on a new phone](restore-your-account.md).
- If your recovery phrase won't work, see ["Invalid recovery phrase"](invalid-recovery-phrase.md).

Anyone who offers to recover your account or your ᴄᴀꜱʜ, especially for a fee, is trying to scam you. See [Scams to watch for](../07-safety-and-scams/common-scams.md).
