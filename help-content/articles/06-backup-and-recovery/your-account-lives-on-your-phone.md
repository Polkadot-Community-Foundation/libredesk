Polkadot has no password and no sign-in. Your account is created on your phone when you choose your username, and it lives there. Only you hold the keys.

## How it works

When you set up, the app creates your account and its recovery phrase on your phone. The keys stay on your phone, in protected hardware.

Because there's no password, there's nothing to reset. If you move to a new phone, your backup or your recovery phrase brings your account back, with your username and your ᴄᴀꜱʜ.

## What this means for you

- Keep your account backed up. On the Google Play version, the app backs it up to Google Drive for you. On the direct download version, write down your recovery phrase. See [Backing up your Polkadot app with Google Drive](back-up-to-google-drive.md) and [Back up without Google Drive](back-up-without-google-drive.md).
- If you lose your phone, your backup or your recovery phrase gets you back in. Without either, nobody can recover your account, including Polkadot.
- Anyone who has your recovery phrase can open your account and spend your ᴄᴀꜱʜ. Polkadot support will never ask for it.
