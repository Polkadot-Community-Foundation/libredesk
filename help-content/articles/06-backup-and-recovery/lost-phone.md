If your phone is lost or stolen, start by changing your Google password, because for most users your Polkadot backup is in your Google Drive. Then restore your account on another phone.

## Do this now

1. Change your Google account password. From another phone or a computer, go to [myaccount.google.com](https://myaccount.google.com), open **Security** and change your password. This keeps whoever has your phone out of your Google Drive, where your Polkadot backup is saved.
2. Sign out of your Google account everywhere else. In the same **Security** section, go to your devices and sign out of the lost phone and any device you don't recognise.
3. Restore your account on another phone. Install Polkadot, tap **Recover here** on the username screen, then choose **Backup from Google Drive** and sign in with your new password. See [Restore your account on a new phone](restore-your-account.md).
4. Check your ᴄᴀꜱʜ. Open your **Pocket** on the new phone. If you see "Balance restored", tap **Update** until it looks right. See [Changing phones and accessing your ᴄᴀꜱʜ](../04-cash-and-payments/changing-phones.md).

If your phone was stolen, you can also lock or erase it remotely with Google's Find Hub.

If your phone is only broken and nobody else has it, you can go straight to step 3.

## If you use the direct download version

The direct download version (for GrapheneOS and phones without Google Play) has no Google Drive backup. Restore your account on another phone with your recovery phrase: tap **Recover here**, then **Import recovery phrase**.

## What can and can't be undone

- With your Google Drive backup or your recovery phrase, you get your account back, with your username and your ᴄᴀꜱʜ.
- Without either, nobody can recover your account, including Polkadot.
- Restoring on a new phone doesn't remove your account from the old one. Anyone who can unlock your old phone may be able to open Polkadot on it. That's why changing your Google password comes first, and why erasing a stolen phone helps.

## Next time
