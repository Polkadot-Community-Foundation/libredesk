To move your account to a new phone, install Polkadot and restore it from your Google Drive backup or your recovery phrase. You'll get your username and your ᴄᴀꜱʜ back.

If your old phone was lost or stolen, start with [I lost my phone](lost-phone.md), which explains why to change your Google password first.

## Start restoring

1. Install Polkadot and open it.
2. Choose a theme and tap **Continue**.
3. On the username screen, don't choose a new username. Tap **Recover here**, next to "Already using Polkadot?".
4. Choose **Backup from Google Drive** or **Import recovery phrase**.

## From your Google Drive backup

This works on the Google Play version.

1. Tap **Backup from Google Drive**.
2. Sign in with the Google account you used before, and allow access to Google Drive.

The app finds your backup and restores your account.

If you see "No backup was found in Google Drive. Recover with your recovery phrase instead.", check that you chose the right Google account. If you see "This backup is damaged and can't be read. Recover with your recovery phrase instead.", use your recovery phrase.

## With your recovery phrase

1. Tap **Import recovery phrase**.
2. On "Restore your account", type the words of your recovery phrase in order. The screen accepts 12 or 24 words.
3. Tap **Restore account**.

If you see "Invalid recovery phrase", see ["Invalid recovery phrase"](invalid-recovery-phrase.md).

## After you restore

- You'll see "Restoring your account" for a moment.
- If your account doesn't have a username yet, you'll see "Account restored" and can choose one. See [Choose your username](../02-username-and-id/choose-a-username.md).
- Open your **Pocket**. If you see "Balance restored", tap **Update** until your ᴄᴀꜱʜ looks right. See [Changing phones and accessing your ᴄᴀꜱʜ](../04-cash-and-payments/changing-phones.md).
