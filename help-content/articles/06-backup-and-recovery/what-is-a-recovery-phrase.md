Your recovery phrase is a list of 12 words that can restore your Polkadot account on any phone. Think of it like a long password.
The app creates it on your phone when you set up, and it's the only way back into your account if your phone is lost or replaced. If you back up with Google Drive, you never have to worry about this, but it's important to understand how it works. 

## How it works

Your account lives on your phone, and only you hold the keys to it. The recovery phrase is those keys written as words. Type the words into the Polkadot app on another phone and you get your account back, with your username and your ᴄᴀꜱʜ.

You don't have to deal with it day to day:

- On the Google Play version, the app backs up your recovery phrase to your Google Drive, encrypted, when you set up. Most people never need to look at it. See [Backing up your Polkadot app with Google Drive](back-up-to-google-drive.md).
- On the direct download version (for GrapheneOS and phones without Google Play), there's no cloud backup, so you need to write the words down yourself. See [Back up without Google Drive](back-up-without-google-drive.md).

You can see your recovery phrase at any time in **Settings** > **Backup** > **View recovery phrase**.

## What this means for you

> [!WARNING]
> Anyone who has your recovery phrase can open your account and spend your ᴄᴀꜱʜ. Polkadot support will never ask for it.

- Keep a copy, somewhere only you can get to.
- Don't take a screenshot of it or store it in notes, chats or email.
- Don't type it into any website or other app. The only place it belongs is the Polkadot app, when you restore your account.
- If you lose your phone and you have neither your recovery phrase nor a Google Drive backup, nobody can get your account back for you, including Polkadot.
