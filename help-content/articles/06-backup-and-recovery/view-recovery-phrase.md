You can see your recovery phrase at any time in **Settings**. You'll need your phone's screen lock to open it.

## View your recovery phrase

1. Tap **Settings** in the dock, then tap **Backup**.
2. Tap **View recovery phrase**.
3. A sheet titled "View Private Key" opens with three reminders. Read them, then tap **Show recovery phrase**. <!-- REVIEW F15d: the sheet title should say "recovery phrase". -->
4. When you see "Verify it's you", unlock with your fingerprint, face, PIN, pattern or password.
5. Make sure nobody can see your screen, then tap **Tap to reveal**.

There's no copy button, so write the words down by hand, in order.

> [!WARNING]
> Anyone who has your recovery phrase can open your account and spend your ᴄᴀꜱʜ. Polkadot support will never ask for it.

Don't take a screenshot. The app reminds you: "Make sure no one can see your screen and do not take a screenshot".

## If it doesn't open

If you see "Couldn't confirm it's you. Try again.", unlock with your screen lock again.
