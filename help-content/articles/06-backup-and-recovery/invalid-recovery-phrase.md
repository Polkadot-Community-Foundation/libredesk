You see "Invalid recovery phrase" and "Please check your recovery phrase" when the words you typed don't make a valid recovery phrase. Usually one word is misspelled or in the wrong place.

A similar message, "Those words don't match your recovery phrase. Try again.", also means the words aren't quite right.

## What to do

1. Tap **Got it**.
2. Compare each word with your written copy, letter by letter.
3. Check the order. The first word on your list goes first.
4. Count the words. Recovery phrases created in Polkadot have 12 words, and the screen also accepts 24.
5. Check for extra spaces, or two words that have run together. If your keyboard changes words as you type, check them again.
6. Tap **Restore account**.

## If it still doesn't work

- On the Google Play version, try restoring from your Google Drive backup instead. See [Restore your account on a new phone](restore-your-account.md).
- Nobody can look up or reset a recovery phrase for you, including Polkadot. See [Can Polkadot recover my account?](can-polkadot-recover-my-account.md)
