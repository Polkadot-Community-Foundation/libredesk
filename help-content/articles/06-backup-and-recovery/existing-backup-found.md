You see this when your Google Drive already holds a backup of a Polkadot account. You can recover that account, or replace its backup with a backup of the account you're using now.

> [!WARNING]
> Replacing a backup can't be undone. If you haven't written down the old account's recovery phrase, you won't be able to get that account or its ᴄᴀꜱʜ back.

## While setting up: "We found an existing backup"

The screen shows when the backup was created.

- To use that account, tap **Recover this backup**.
- To start a new account instead, tap **Replace with a new backup**. You'll see "Replacing this backup is permanent." If you've written down the old account's recovery phrase, tick **I've written down the recovery phrase for the old account.** and tap **Replace backup**.

## In Settings: "Backup conflict"

You'll see "You have another account backed up on Google Drive."

1. Tap **Override existing backup**.
2. On "Backup found", tap **Remove and create new backup**.
3. Tick **I fully understand the risk**, then tap **Create a new backup**.

To keep the old backup, tap **Cancel** at any step.

## Which should I choose?

- If the backup is from an account you still want, keep it. During setup, tap **Recover this backup**.
- Replace it only if you've written down the old account's recovery phrase, or you're sure you don't need that account or its ᴄᴀꜱʜ.
