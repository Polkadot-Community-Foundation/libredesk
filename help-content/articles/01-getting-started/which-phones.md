Polkadot works on Android phones running Android 10 or later with a locked bootloader. That includes Android with or without Google Play services, and GrapheneOS.

| Your phone | Can it use Polkadot? | Which to install |
|---|---|---|
| Android 10 or later, with Google Play | Yes | Google Play version |
| Android 10 or later, without Google Play services | Yes | Direct download version |
| GrapheneOS, with a locked bootloader | Yes | Direct download version |
| Rooted, or with an unlocked bootloader | No | |
| Other custom ROMs | No | |
| Emulators | No | |

Polkadot is available for Android phones, in English, more languages coming soon. 

## Why some phones can't use it

When you choose a username, the app checks that your phone is genuine and hasn't been modified. This device verification is what lets the app give every account free usage. Rooted phones, unlocked bootloaders, other custom ROMs and emulators can't pass it, and the app shows "We couldn't verify this device". See ["We couldn't verify this device"](../02-username-and-id/device-cant-be-verified.md).
