You may see "You're almost there" when lots of people are signing up at the same time. 
New accounts wait in a short queue, which protects the service from bots and fake mass sign-ups. 

## What to do

1. Look under "Your position" to see your place in the queue.
2. Wait for your turn. When it comes, you don't need to do anything, the Polkadot app will continue setting everything up for you :)
