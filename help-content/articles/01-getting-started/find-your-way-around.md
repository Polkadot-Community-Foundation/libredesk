The dock at the bottom of the screen takes you around the app. Its five icons have no labels. From left to right:

| Icon | What it opens |
|---|---|
| Chats | Your conversations and message requests |
| Pocket | Your ᴄᴀꜱʜ purse and your ID card |
| The scan button (the raised one in the middle) | The camera, to scan a QR code, and search, to find someone by username |
| Settings | Your theme, notifications, ᴄᴀꜱʜ Privacy Mode, backup and blocked contacts, plus Legal & Support |
| Network Status | How well the app is connected right now |

The dock shows on the Chats, Pocket and Settings screens. When you open a chat or another screen, go back to see the dock again.

## Chats

Your conversations live here. When someone new writes to you, a "New requests" row appears at the top. Tap **+** at the top of the screen to find someone by username.

- [Start a chat](../03-chats-and-calls/start-a-chat.md)

- [Make voice and video calls](../03-chats-and-calls/voice-video-calls.md)

## Pocket

Your Pocket holds your ᴄᴀꜱʜ purse and your ID card.

- [Your Pocket and ᴄᴀꜱʜ purse](../04-cash-and-payments/pocket-and-purse.md)

## The scan button

Tap the scan button to open the camera and scan someone's QR code, to add them or pay them. To look someone up by username, swipe up on it, or tap it and then tap **Search**.

- [Start a chat](../03-chats-and-calls/start-a-chat.md)

## Settings

A dot on the Settings icon means your account isn't backed up yet. Your app version is at the bottom of the Settings screen.

## Network Status

Tap Network Status to see how the app is connected, and tap it again to close. The small rings at the top right of the screen show the same thing at a glance.
