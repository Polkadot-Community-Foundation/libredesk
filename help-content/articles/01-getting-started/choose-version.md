Most people should use the Google Play version. The direct download version is for GrapheneOS and other phones without Google Play services, and for people who'd rather not rely on Google.

## How the versions differ

| | Google Play version | Direct download version |
|---|---|---|
| Where to get it | Google Play | [direct download link] |
| Needs Google Play services | Yes | No |
| How your phone is checked | Through Google Play | By your phone's own hardware |
| Backup | Encrypted backup to Google Drive, on by default | You write down your recovery phrase |
| Notifications when the app is closed | Yes | No |
| Ads, analytics and tracking | None | None |

Both versions have the same chats, calls, ᴄᴀꜱʜ and ID card.

## Which should I choose?

Choose the Google Play version if your phone has Google Play. Your backup and notifications are handled for you.

Choose the direct download version if you use GrapheneOS or another phone without Google Play services, or you'd rather not rely on Google. Write down your recovery phrase, because there's no cloud backup. See [Back up without Google Drive](../06-backup-and-recovery/back-up-without-google-drive.md). Open the app from time to time to check for new messages.

## Switching versions

Your account isn't tied to either version. To move to the other one, install it and restore your account with your recovery phrase.
