Polkadot is a free app for private messages, voice and video calls, and ᴄᴀꜱʜ, which is digital cash denominated in US dollars.

## What you can do

- Chat with people. Every message is end-to-end encrypted, and photos and videos are encrypted before they leave your phone.
- Make encrypted, peer-to-peer voice and video calls from any chat.
- Send ᴄᴀꜱʜ to anyone you chat with, right inside the conversation. There are no fees to send ᴄᴀꜱʜ, and $1 ᴄᴀꜱʜ is worth $1.
- Show your ID card, a QR code with your username on it, so people can find you, message you and pay you.

## How it works

Your account lives on your phone, and only you hold the access. Unlike traditional chat apps and payment services you've likely used before there is no company or entity which holds access to your chat, data or money.  For the first time - you are not the product.   

The app creates your account when you choose your username. It also creates a recovery phrase, which can bring your account back on another phone.

The app runs on Polkadot, a public network no single company controls. Messages are delivered over Polkadot's public network, not a company server.

The Polkadot Community Foundation publishes the app. It's built from open-source code, and it has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
