Your username is how people find you, message you and pay you. You choose it when you set up Polkadot, and it's yours for good, so take a moment over it.

## What a username looks like

A username is 6–29 lowercase letters, then a dot and a two-digit number, for example `jake.23`. When you tell people your username, include the number.

## Choose your username

1. On "Pick a username", type the name you want in the "Enter username" box.
2. Read the message under the box:
   - "It's yours!" means you can have it.
   - "Taken. Try another.", "Try another." or "This username was just claimed. Try another." means you need to choose a different name.
   - "Digits taken. Try again." means that number has gone. Try again.
   - "This username was just claimed. We picked a new number for you." means someone got there first, so the app gave you a new number. Check you're happy with it.
3. Tap **Get username**. Until your name is long enough, the button says "Minimum 6 characters".

Once you have a username, people can find you by searching for it.

> [!INFO]
> This will be your username for good. You can't change it later.

Claiming a username is free. Your phone goes through device verification first. See [What is device verification?](device-verification.md)

## If something goes wrong

- If you see "Couldn't check this username. Try again.", check your internet connection and try again.
- If the app says it couldn't verify your phone, see ["We couldn't verify this device"](device-cant-be-verified.md).

## If you restored an account without a username

If you restore an account that doesn't have a username yet, you'll see "Account restored" and "Claim a username", just follow the same steps.
