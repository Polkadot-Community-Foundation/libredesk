Your ID card shows your username and a QR code. Anyone who scans the code can add you as a contact or send you ᴄᴀꜱʜ.

## Open your ID card

1. Tap **Pocket** in the dock.
2. Tap your ID card.

You'll see "Show this QR to someone who wants to add you as a contact or send you ᴄᴀꜱʜ", with your username and QR code underneath.

To get to the code faster, tap the QR icon on the ID card in your Pocket.

<!-- screenshot: the ID card screen -->

## Let someone scan it

1. Open your ID card.
2. Ask the other person to tap the scan button in the middle of their dock and point their camera at your QR code.

A chat with you opens on their phone, ready for them to send you a message request.

## Copy your username

Tap the copy icon next to your username. Then paste it wherever you like, for example into a message.

## Share it in another app

1. Open your ID card.
2. Tap the share icon at the top right.
3. Choose an app.

They'll get your QR code and an invite message with a link to download Polkadot. If sharing doesn't work, you'll see "Couldn't share your ID. Try again."
