No. Once you claim a username, it's yours for good and can't be changed. This helps stop spam and impersonation. 

## What you can do instead

- Share your exact username, including the dot and the number at the end, so people find the right person.
- Share your ID card. Anyone who scans its QR code finds you without typing anything. See [Use your ID card](id-card.md).
