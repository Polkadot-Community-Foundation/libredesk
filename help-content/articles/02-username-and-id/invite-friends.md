To invite a friend, share your ID card. They get a link to download Polkadot and your username, so they can find you once they're set up.

## Send an invite

1. Tap **Pocket** in the dock, then tap your ID card.
2. Tap the share icon at the top right.
3. Choose how to send it, for example a messaging app or email.

Your friend gets your QR code and a short message with a download link and your username.

If you're together, you can skip the link. Once they've installed Polkadot, they can scan the QR code on your ID card straight from your screen.

## Once they've installed Polkadot

1. They set up the app and choose a username. See [Set up Polkadot](../01-getting-started/set-up-polkadot.md).
2. They find you by searching for your username or scanning your QR code.
3. They send you a message request. When you accept it, you can chat, call and send each other ᴄᴀꜱʜ.

Polkadot works on Android 10 or later. See [Which phones can use Polkadot?](../01-getting-started/which-phones.md)
